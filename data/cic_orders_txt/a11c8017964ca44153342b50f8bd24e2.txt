     4. To provide copy of noting & correspondence and remarks of Assistant

     6. Please provide copy of noting & correspondence with MHRD and remark

     the show cause notice.

     7. Furnish the action taken report & noting on reply of appellant letter

     8. To provide pointwise & parawise complete information on appellant

                                       2


       11. What is the procedure followed to establish that allotment of staff
      quarter from 2010 to 2013. Also provide the list of staff which is necessary
      to allotted staff quarter with proper reason."



The CPIO/ MHRD furnished reply to the appellant on 21.05.2019 against point
Nos. 1 & 2 of the RTI application and with regard to point No. 3 to 11; CPIO
transferred the RTI application under 
section 6(3)
 of RTI Act, 2005 to Shri Dinesh
Kumar PIO, National Bal Bhavan (NBB).



Subsequently, CPIO/NBB on 18.06.2019 furnished reply against point No. 3 to 11
of the RTI application.



Being dissatisfied with non-receipt of desired information on point no.2,3,5,7,9,10
& 11 of the RTI Application, the appellant filed a First Appeal dated 01.07.2019
which was forwarded to the FAA, NBB. FAA, NBB upheld the reply of CPIO vide
order dated 22.08.2019 and also provided certain additional information on
various service-related issues raised by the Appellant and for point no.2 provided
the relevant inputs.



Feeling aggrieved and dissatisfied with the FAA's order, appellant approached the
Commission with the instant Second Appeal praying for a committee to be
constituted in the matter to provide justice to him.



Relevant Facts emerging during Hearing:

The following were present:-



Appellant: Present through audio conference.

Respondent: Dinesh Kumar, SO & CPIO, National Bal Bhawan present through
audio conference.



The Appellant stated that he has not received the desired information till date
and that he is aggrieved with the constant harassment caused to him in his
service matter and also by the action of the Respondent office and National Bal
Bhawan in merely shifting the onus of providing the information to each other. He
further stated that all his contentions are contained in his written submissions
sent to the Commission prior to the hearing.



                                         3


 The CPIO submitted that all the available information has been provided to the
Appellant as can be evinced from their reply dated 18.06.2019 and even the FAA
provided additional information vide her order dated 22.08.2019.



Decision

The Commission observes from a perusal of the facts on record that the CPIO,
National Bal Bhawan has provided a detailed reply to points no.3-11 of the RTI
Application, while MHRD (Respondent office) provided a factual reply to point no.
1 & 2 of the RTI Application. Subsequently, based on the grounds of First Appeal
of the Appellant, the FAA, National Bal Bhawan provided additional information
while also clarifying the factual position explained by MHRD to the Appellant for
the information sought for at point no.2 of the RTI Application.



It is pertinent to note that across the length and breadth of the Appellant's
written submission dated 02.06.2021, he has narrated the service-related
grievances harboured by him over the course of time. He has further challenged
the reply received on the instant RTI Application through speculative arguments
and hypothesis, and adjudicating on the merits of administrative grievances is not
within the scope of jurisdiction of the Commission under the 
RTI Act
.



In view of the foregoing, no action is warranted in the matter. The Appellant is
however advised to agitate his administrative grievances before the appropriate
forum.



                     The appeal is disposed of accordingly.
