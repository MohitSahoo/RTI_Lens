Decision:

In the light of the facts of the case and the submissions made by both the parties,
the Commission at the outset observes that the reply provided in Second Appeal




                                                                         Page 21 of 23

 No.     CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/628264,            CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/649738            and
CIC/PMOIN/A/2022/617566, is in accordance with the provisions of the 
RTI Act,
2005
 since as per the provisions of the Act only such information that is held and
available with a public authority can be provided.



The Commission further observes that the reply provided in Second Appeal No.
CIC/MHOME/A/2022/624716, is in accordance with the provisions of the 
RTI Act,
2005
. Hence, no further intervention of the Commission is required in the instant
matter. For redressal of his grievance, if any, the Appellant is advised to approach
an appropriate forum.

Be that as it may, before parting with the case, the Commission observes that the
Respondent, Shri Manoj Kasana, Second in Command (DM) Division, has neither
participated in the hearing nor has given any plausible explanation for his
absence. This is found to be unacceptable and he is cautioned that such conduct
will not be accepted, nor absence from hearing excused, without any justifiable
explanation, in future.

However,        in      Second     Appeal       No.     CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/663496,
CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/663495 and CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/652638, the reply
provided by the Respondent seems to be inadequate and evasive and even after
receipt of the hearing notice from the Commission, the Respondent did not take
any initiative to send a proper response to the Appellant. The Respondent is
advised to be more proactive in future while handling RTI matters and readily
provide information to the information seekers. Further in Second Appeal No.
CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/663496, the Appellant in point no. 3 and 4 is seeking
information regarding his own APAR. 
In the context the Commission would like to
reply upon judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in the decision of 
Dev
Dutt vs Union of India & Ors
 on 12 May, 2008, Civil Appeal No. 7631 OF 2002,
wherein it has been held as under
   "19. In our opinion, every entry in the A.C.R. of a public servant must be
   communicated to him within a reasonable period, whether it is a poor, fair,
   average, good or very good entry. This is because non-communication of such an
   entry may adversely affect the employee in two ways : (1) Had the entry been
   communicated to him he would know about the assessment of his work and
   conduct by his superiors, which would enable him to improve his work in future (2)
   He would have an opportunity of making a representation against the entry if he
   feels it is unjustified, and pray for its upgradation.
 
Hence non-communication of
   an entry is arbitrary, and it has been held by the Constitution Bench decision of
   this Court in 
Maneka Gandhi vs. Union of India
 (supra) that arbitrariness violates
   
Article 14
 of the Constitution. 20. Thus it is not only when there is a bench mark
   but in all cases that an entry (whether it is poor, fair, average, good or very good)
   must be communicated to a public servant, otherwise there is violation of the
   principle of fairness, which is the soul of natural justice. Even an outstanding
   entry should be communicated since that would boost the morale of the employee
   and make him work harder."




In view of the above judgement ACRs/ APARs ought to be disclosed to the
employee. Thus, in view of the above, Shri S Y Kamble, CPIO and CAO, Heavy

  Water Plant, Manuguru is directed to re-examine the said RTI applications and
 provide a revised response in accordance with the provisions of the Act to the
 Appellant by 30.05.2023 under intimation to the Commission.



 Regarding Second Appeal No CIC/HWPMG/A/2022/645320, the Appellant is not
 satisfied with the reply provided by the Respondent. The Commission is of the view
 that the generic information regarding charter of duties should normally be
 disclosed. Therefore, the Commission deems it fit to direct the Respondent to
 revisit the RTI Application and furnish a revised reply to the Appellant by providing
 the information as available in their records, within four weeks from the date of
 receipt of this order.



 Notwithstanding the above position, the Commission strongly recommends that
 the Respondent public authority should have a robust grievance redressal
 mechanism so that employees of the organization have an appropriate channel to
 get their grievances addressed internally, within the organization.



 With the above directions, the instant Second Appeals stand disposed off
 accordingly.
