RTI Act, 2005
 and the information requested must be
provided by public authority until unless conditions under 
section 8(1)(b)

of RTI Act, 2005 are fulfilled i.e. 'information which has been expressly

disclosure of which may constitute contempt of court'

So, there are two mandatory conditions to be fulfilled under 
section
8(1)(b)
 of RTI Act, 2005 for denial of information and those two
conditions are:

i. expressly forbidden to be published by any court of law, and
ii. the disclosure of which may constitute contempt of court

It's easy to follow from the section that only that information which has
been expressly forbidden by any court of law is exempted and mere
pendency of information, of some related case, before a court does not
signifies its exemption. So, one of the prerequisite for application of

Section 8 (1) (b)
 is an expressed order from any court of law exempting
publication of the information asked for.

Decision:



In furtherance of the hearing proceedings and the submissions made by both
the parties during the hearing observed that identical issue has already been
heard and decided by the Coordinate bench of the Commission in Appeal No.
CIC/DOFWL/A/2022/112857 dated 30.11.2022 wherein it was specifically
observed that:



"Keeping in view the facts of the case and the submissions made by both
parties and after perusal of the documents available on record, the Commission
observes that this is a case of severe infringement of the RTI Law as it involves
the violation of the 
RTI Act
's twin objectives of brining transparency and
accountability in the system. The Commission observes that no information was
furnished to the appellant by the CPIO and he did not comply with the order of

 the CIC repeatedly. This amounts to making mockery of the 
RTI Act
. The
Commission, therefore, directs the registry of this bench to issue a Show Cause

 should not be initiated against them for not

a copy of this order."



The Commission observes that the issue of non-compliance of FAA order was
already raised by the Complainant at the time of hearing of his second appeal
and the coordinate bench of the Commission has also issued show-cause
notice to the Respondent. The Complainant in the present complaint has again
raised identical issues which have already been dealt with by the Commission.
The Respondent cannot be show-caused twice for same lapse.



Therefore, similar issue cannot be adjudicated again. Hence, no intervention of
the Commission is required in the matter.
