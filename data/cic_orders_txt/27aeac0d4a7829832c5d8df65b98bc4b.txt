1.      The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeals filed in the
month of May & June 2018 include non-receipt of the information as mentioned in
para 5.3 raised by the appellant through his RTI application dated 19.2.2018.



                                                                            Page 1 of 9


 2.    Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an online application
dated 19.02.2018 under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the
Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), Prime Minister Office, New Delhi,
seeking aforesaid information. The RTI application was transferred to the CPIO of
DGE & DoFS which was further transferred to the 22 banks including above
mentioned 9 banks on 26.02.2018. The CPIOs had furnished point wise replies vide
their respective letters to the appellant which are at variance from each other.
Dissatisfied with this, the appellant filed first appeal dated 30.03.2018. The First
Appellate Authoritiesdisposed of the first appeals filed by the above mentioned nine
respondent banks. Aggrieved by this, the appellant filed nine second appeals in the
month of May & June 2018 before this Commission which are under consideration.
As all these appeals have arisen out of the one RTI application dated 19.2.2018
addressed to PMO and the second appeals have been filed against the aforementioned
9 banks. Therefore, all these appeals are dealt with in a common order.



3.       The appellant has filed the instant appeals inter alia on the grounds that
complete information was not furnished by the concerned CPIOs andno personal
hearing was given by the FAAs. The appellant also stated that exemptions claimed by
the respondents would not be applicable in this case as the respondent had
completely ignored the larger public interest involved in this matter. The appellant
requested the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the complete information,
impose penalty on the CPIOs under the 
RTI Act
 for wilful denial of the information
and any other relief as the Commission may deemed fit in accordance with the law.



4.    The point-wise reply had already been furnished by the respondents vide their
respective letters wherein they had denied most of the information under 
Section 7
(9)
 and 
Section 8 (1) (d) (e)
 & (j) of the 
RTI Act
. The FAAs had agreed with the
views taken by the CPIOs.

5.1.   The appellant inter alia submitted that he received responses from the
respondent banks. However, not all the respondents had brought out the requisite
information through their replies. The appellant further submitted that the
Commission had directed for disclosure of the information earlier in similar appeals
vide order dated 16.11.2018. Therefore, the respondent banks were under an
obligation not only to comply with the Commission's directions but also disclose
complete information as per provisions of 
RTI Act
 with utmost transparency and
accountability.



5.2.   The respondents while defending their case inter alia submitted that they had
provided the total number loan accounts with sanctioned limit above Rs.50 crores.
However, in respect of the remaining information sought in the RTI application they
had sought recourse to various exemption provisions under 
section 8
 of the RTI Act
as well assection 7 (9) of the 
RTI Act
.

banks to whom the RTI application was forwarded i.e. Vijaya Bank had furnished

6.    The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing all the parties and perusal of records, noted that the reply given by the
respondents nos. 1 to 9 were evasive and incomplete. However, their sister bank
which is now a part of Bank of Baroda, i.e. Vijaya Bank, have given appropriate
information on the RTI application as mentioned in para 5.3. The appellant has
nowhere sought the list of names of account holders or any personal details
whatsoever relating to the same. Hence, the exemptions claimed under 
section 8
 of
the RTI Act were unfounded. Moreover, the information as provided by the Vijaya
Bank included the cumulative data and disclosure of the information on those lines
would not attract the limitation underlined in 
section 7 (9)
 of RTI Act. Accordingly,

                                                                              Page 6 of 9

 the respondent nos. 1 to 9 are directed to revisit the RTI application and provide
information on the lines of the aforementioned reply given by the then Vijaya Bank
to the appellant within four weeks of the receipt of this order. With the above
observations and directions and all 9 appeals are disposed of.
