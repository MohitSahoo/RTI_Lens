1.    The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated 22.03.2019
include non-receipt of the following information raised by the appellant through his RTI
application dated 14.08.2018 and first appeal dated 17.10.2018:-

      The applicant has been involuntarily vacated from the banking service and in
       this regard, an appeal was preferred by the applicant which is pending disposal
       before the competent authority. It is not known what just & fair reason prompted
       the authority to with held the Form-16(Part-A & B) of the applicant pertaining
       to the Financial Year 2017-18 (AY 2018-19), which ought to have been provided
       to the applicant proactively. However, despite request letter dated 19.06.2018
       sent by hand through applicant's father which was followed by the registered
       (AD) request letter dated 02.07.2018, the applicant has been deprived of the

2.          Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
14.08.2018 under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO), State Bank of India, Solan, seeking aforesaid information.
The CPIO vide letter dated 15.09.2018 replied to the appellant. Dissatisfied with that, the
appellant filed first appeal dated 17.10.2018.The First Appellate Authority vide order
dated 21.11.2018 disposed of the first appeal. Aggrieved by that, the appellant filed a
second appeal dated 22.03.2019 before this Commission which is under consideration.

                                                                                       Page 2 of 5


 3.     The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 22.03.2019 inter alia on the grounds
that the CPIO deliberately withheld the information; that the FAA did not grant him an
opportunity to hearing during disposal of the first appeal; that the information was
directly related to life and liberty; that the omission on part of the CPIO had created
conscious obstruction of information. The appellant requested the Commission to direct
the CPIO to provide the complete information and take necessary action as per
provisions under 
Section 20 (1)
, 
20 (2)
 of the RTI Act.



4.      The CPIO replied vide letter dated 15.09.2018 replied that no salary was paid for
the period 2017-18, hence, no Form-16 was generated; that the lists in point nos 2 and 3
of the RTI application were provided; for the promotion year 2017-18 no written test was
held and primary eligibility criteria was AARF of the past 3 years and whosoever did not
fall in the prescribed criteria was left out from the promotion process of the year 2017-
18; the written test was not held for the promotion year 2017-18 owing to the merger of
associate banks; copies of the relevant notings were exempted under provisions of

section 8 (1) (d)
 and (e) of the 
RTI Act
, etc. The FAA held that the information sought
by the appellant did not include any commercial confidence/trade secrets or intellectual
property therefore was not exempted under the 
RTI Act
. The FAA directed the CPIO to
re-look into the matter and provide proper reply to the appellant within 15 days.



5.      The appellant represented by Dr. Ashwani Sharma, Counsel and on behalf of the
respondent Shri Sunil Choudhary, CPIO, State Bank of India, Solan, attended the hearing
through video conference.



5.1.    The appellant inter alia submitted that the information provided by the respondent
was incomplete. The appellant alleged that the list of candidates for promotion in 2017
and 2018 was prepared in an arbitrary manner. Therefore, he had sought the relevant
notings in respect of the promotions in order to establish bad governance within their
bank. The appellant further stated that the respondent did not disburse his complete
salary and withheld salary for the months of March to July 2017.



                                                                                    Page 3 of 5


 5.2.   The respondent while defending their case inter alia submitted that the appellant
had voluntarily retired on 23.07.2017, hence, no question of promotion in 2017 and 2018
arose. Further, the details in respect of the promotions in 2018 were made available to
the appellant vide CPIO's letter dated 15.09.2018.



6.     The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
both the parties and perusal of records, observes that the reply given by the respondent
was incomplete and evasive. The appellant had sought information relating to
promotions in the bank in 2017 and 2018. The respondent claimed that the appellant
having retired the information was not relevant. However, principles of transparency and
accountability demand that the information relating to promotions be published by the
respondent authority and may also be placed in their web portal in compliance of 
section
4 (1)
 of the RTI Act. In view of the above, the respondent is directed to re-visit the RTI
application and provide information in respect of point nos 5 and 6 of the RTI
application to the appellant within three weeks from date of receipt of this order. With
the aforementioned observations and directions, the appeal is disposed of.
