                                      ORDER



Information sought:

The Appellant filed an RTI Application dated 18.04.2019 seeking information on
the following fifteen points:



   1) "The date of laying of Point No. 131of PURI yard of KUR division.

   2) The Name & designation of official present at the time of laying of point No. 131of
      PURI yard.

   3) The details of maintenance activity carried out over point No. 131after laying to
      till 18.10Hrs Dt.05.11.2018.

   4) The name & designation of official inspected point No. 131of PURI yard on &
      after laying.

   5) The details of traffic passed over point No.131of PURI yard after laying to till
      18.10Hrs of Dt.05.11.2018.

   6) The details of track restoration activity carried out over point No. 131of PUI yard
      on Dt.05.11.2019.

   7) The details of versine of point No. 131of PURI yard on Dt.05.11.2018.

   8) The designation of official in hierarchy responsible for maintenance of track right
      from Track Maintainer in divisional level.

   9) The authority based on which Para 9.02.(b).(vi) of Accident Manual -2011 of East
      Coast Railway violated vide Sr.DSO/KURs letter No.KUR/SFY/PUI
      Yard/04054/18477/18 Dt 06.11.2018.

   10) The safety tolerance of variation of versines on 1in 8.5& 1 in 21 Turn outs on
      6Mtr chord.

   11) The correlation between maintenance of track parameters & safety tolerance
      available if any in the context of derailments.

   12) The authority which reflects condemned bronze wearing piece of side bearers
      has no role in negotiating the curved track.

   13) The authority which reflects more than 1mm wheel gauge allowed on same axle
      of wheel set & has no effect on derailment.

   14) The authority which reflects more than 0.5mm wheel dia allowed on wheels of
      same axle of the bogie & has no effect on derailment.

   15) All the copies of Note Sheet & documents from Dt. 20.02.2019 to Dt. 16.04.2019
      in connection with Notice of DRM (Engg) No. Engg/Accdt/RRD/2019/
      Dt.16.04.2019."



The CPIO vide letter dated 24.05.2019, enclosed two letters endorsed by Sr.
Divisional Mech. Engineer on 24.05.2019 and Sr. DEN/HQ/KUR dated

17.05.2019 which categorically and individually contains point-wise information
as sought in the instant RTI Application. Being dissatisfied, the Appellant filed a
First Appeal dated 19.06.2019. The First Appellate Authority vide order dated
20.06.2019, upheld the CPIO's reply.



Grounds for Second Appeal:

The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of
unsatisfactory reply furnished by the Respondent. He requested the Commission to
direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.



Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:

In order to ensure social distancing and prevent the spread of the pandemic,
COVID-19, the instant hearing is being scheduled through audio conference after
informing both the parties.



The Appellant stated that he has received information on point no. 1 of the RTI
Application but not on the remaining points of the RTI Application.



The Respondent submitted that available and relevant information has been
provided to the Appellant. He reiterated the contents of the written submission
dated 01.07.2021. He further submitted that a copy of the written submission has
also been marked to the Appellant's email address.



The Appellant interjected to state that he has received only the covering letters but
not the attachments/annexures/enclosures to it.



Upon queried by the Commission as to whether the Respondent can accommodate
the Appellant to inspect the relevant records, Shri Ranjan Mohanty replied in
affirmative.

Decision:

Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that though a satisfactory reply has
been provided by the Respondent, the Appellant is not satisfied with the same.
However, in order to allay the apprehensions of the Appellant, the Commission
deems it expedient to direct the present CPIO to facilitate inspection of relevant
documents/records as sought in the instant RTI Application on a mutually decided
date and time duly intimated to the Appellant telephonically and in writing. Copy
of documents, if desired be provided to the Appellant, free of cost upto 25 pages
and beyond those, prescribed fees shall be charged as per Rule 4 of the RTI Rules,
2012. The CPIO is further directed that he shall ensure that any information that
falls under the exemption clauses i.e., 
Section 8
 and 
9
 of the RTI Act should be
adequately redacted/blackened out. The said direction should be complied with, by
the CPIO within 60 days from the date of receipt of this order and a compliance
report of the same be duly sent to the Commission, enumerating the details of
documents inspected and copy of documents provided.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
