                        CIC/BDRSF/A/2019/106193
The instant case arises out of proceedings in S.I. LDCE-2012 and rejection of
Sh. Suresh Kumar declaring him medically unfit on account of inadequate
height. The appellant herein, being the legal counsel of the said Suresh Kumar
claims that her client has been victimised and wrongly categorized as medically
unfit and records of his actual height have been manipulated as part of unfair
and discriminatory practice of the respondent organization. It appears that the
appellant had filed another case which had been heard earlier and decided by
the CIC on 26.03.2018. The appellant claims that CPIO, BSF had given a false
statement during the hearing.



The PIO, BSF vide reply dated 04.10.2018 provided a detailed reply to the
appellant informing her that her client, who is currently employed with BSF,
had been provide ample opportunity to access information from appropriate
official channels and had been duly apprised of the updated status with
respect to his queries and why he was considered as medically unfit in the
specific departmental examination.



Not satisfied with the reply, the appellant filed First Appeal dated 16.10.2018.
The FAA/IG/BSF vide order dated 15.11.2018 denied disclosure of information
to the appellant, apprising her that the respondent organization is exempt from
the purview of 
RTI Act
 vide 
Section 24
 of the RTI Act.



Thus, aggrieved due to denial of information, the appellant has filed the instant
Second Appeal.



Proceedings during hearing:

Due to nation-wide lockdown being observed, to prevent the spread of the
pandemic of COVID-19, hearings are being conducted through audio
conference.



The appellant - Sh. Suresh Kumar himself participated in the hearing on being
contacted on his mobile telephone. He submitted that he is presently employed
as a washerman in BSF and was a victim of discrimination at the hands of the
respondent, and declared medically unfit, unfairly. He further stated that he
had filed an earlier Second Appeal on the same subject which was registered as
case no. CIC/BDRSF/A/2017/143134 and heard by the predecessor Bench of
Shri. Yashovardhan Azad, decided vide order dated 26.03.2018. It has been
submitted by the appellant that he was unable to understand the submissions
 made by the respondent during the hearing on 26.03.2018 since the
proceedings were in English. Hence, he filed the aforesaid two cases agitating
the same issue once again. The appellant has sent another order dated
05.05.2016 in case no. CIC/VS/A/2014/003891/SB whereby his case had
been adjudicated even earlier than 2018.



Decision

In the light of the averments of the appellant, the Commission notes that the
appellant has filed repeated RTI cases on the same issue. Though the issue had
been adjudicated vide order dated 05.05.2016, he had not mentioned the same
during hearing held on 26.03.2018. It is noted that the decision dated
26.03.2018 was passed in Hindi and all submissions are also in Hindi,
contrary to the appellant's contention that the respondent's submissions made
in English could not be deciphered by him at the relevant point of time. It is
further noted that after discussing all the relevant submissions placed forth by
both parties the erstwhile Bench had dismissed the case observing that no case
of corruption or violation of human rights could be established by the
appellant. Hence, this case cannot be entertained by the Commission under

RTI Act
, due to the bar of 
Section 24(1)
 of the RTI Act.



It is an established factual position that the Respondent public authority is
enlisted in 
Second Schedule of the RTI Act
 and as such, exempt from
provisions of this Act under 
Section 24
 of the Act. The only exception carved
out to the exemption is wherein information sought relates to allegations of
corruption/human rights violations and approval to disclosure is accorded by
this Commission. The terms "allegation of corruption" and "violation of human
rights" are not defined in the Act, it is thus open for the Commission to decide
the veracity of allegations on both counts on a case to case basis. The
allegations of corruption and human rights violation should be construed to
mean verifiable allegations, in other words, a mere allegation or perception of
an individual about corruption or human rights violation is not sufficient in the
absence of any supporting material that proves such a charge in its evidentiary
value has strength. It is a well settled proposition that every RTI applicant who
utters the word 'corruption‟ or „violation of human rights‟ does not become
entitled to get information from public authorities exempted under 
Section
24(1)
 of the RTI Act. The onus of substantiating the allegation of corruption
and human rights violation lies on the RTI applicant and 'perception' is
certainly no ground to agitate for right to information under the proviso
to 
Section 24(1)
 of the RTI Act. The Commission is convinced that facts of
the instant case fail to fulfill either of the exceptions, viz. corruption or human
rights violation and hence these appeals are barred by operation of the 
Section
24
 of the RTI Act.

 The Commission further notes that the appellant himself has pointed out that
the aforementioned appeals admittedly deal with the same subject matter i.e.
the appellant having been declared medically unfit by the Medical Board on not
meeting the height requirement. Two appeals dealing with the same subject
matter have already been decided by the aforementioned order/s dated
05.05.2016 and 26.03.2018 by different Bench/es. The issue thus stands
adjudicated already by predecessor Bench/es of this Commission and hence
these appeals dealing with the same subject matter cannot be adjudicated
afresh, being barred by the principle of constructive res judicata.
The appellant is advised to refrain from filing any more RTI cases on the same
subject matter, which will not be entertained any further, considering that the
issue has already been decided previously.



The appeals are thus dismissed.
