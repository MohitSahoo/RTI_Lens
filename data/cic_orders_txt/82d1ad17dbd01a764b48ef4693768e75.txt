1.          The issues under consideration i.e. the reliefs sought by the complainant in her
complaint dated 07.09.2018 due to alleged non-supply of information vide her RTI
application dated 13.05.2018 are as under:-

     (i)       Impose maximum penalty on the concerned CPIO as per sub-section (1) of
               
section 20
 of the RTI Act.

     (ii)      Order tax authorities to conduct an enquiry into possible involvement of
               concerned Branch Manager for helping in corrupt acts of surviving partners
               of firm and nexus between them which has resulted in prolonged harassment
               by CPIO in dealing with the complainant's RTI application.

                                                                                   Page 1 of 6


 2.          Succinctly facts of the case are that the complainant filed an RTI application
dated 13.05.2018, under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the
Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), Bank of Baroda, Regional Office,
Bharatpur, seeking inter alia the following information:-



     (i)       Whether operations on all partnership accounts on death of partner of firm are
               stayed as soon as intimation of death of partner is received by Bank. Firm has
               no clause in deed that remaining surviving partners can carry on business.

     (ii)      What happens to FDs, overdraft account, BGs issued by firm. Whether bank
               issues any notice to firm to settle BGs and overdraft amount. Whether
               encashment of FDs stayed till firm clear its liabilities.

     (iii)     If remaining surviving partners enter new partnership deed and form new
               firm then, is it compulsory to close bank account of old firm and open new
               account for new firm?

     (iv)      If surviving partners introduce clause in new partnership deed that it will
               continue to operate bank account of old firm then, is it acceptable to bank as
               per their rules and regulations?

     (v)       What rules and regulations are formed by bank to protect interest of legal
               heirs of deceased partners?

     (vi)      If new firm with new partnership deed is formed, does bank asks it to obtain
               new PAN number?

     (vii) Does rules of bank allow new firm to operate bank account of old firm with
               old PAN number?

     (viii) If yes, what documents are required by bank to allow new firm to do so?

     (ix)      The complainant is daughter of deceased partner Shri Jagdish Prasad
               Agarwal, Firm M/s Jagdish Prasad Agarwal (name of new firm) is allowed by
               Bank of Baroda, Manu Marg Branch, Alwar, Rajasthan to continue to operate
               all bank accounts of old firm M/s Jagdish Prasad Agarwal. No new PAN


                                                                                    Page 2 of 6

               number is obtained by new firm. Explain detailed procedure under bank rules
              so that the complainant can get proper guidance by bank.

     (x)      Explain the detailed procedure formulated by bank for making complaint
              under various policies like vigilance, whistle blower, public interest
              disclosure grievance handing etc.

     (xi)     Information on all bank accounts of all firms in which Shri Jagdish Prasad
              Agarwal was partner held in the respondent's Manu Marg Branch, Alwar as
              on date of his death i.e. 08.08.2016 shall be furnished.

     (xii) Information on all FDs of firm as on 08.08.2016 and details of all FDs in
              name of firm encashed after that shall be given to the complainant.



           The CPIO replied on 22.05.2018. Dissatisfied with the response of the CPIO, the
complainant filed first appeal dated 14.06.2018. The First Appellate Authority disposed
of the first appeal vide order dated 16.07.2018.Aggrieved by this, the complainant filed
complaint dated 07.09.2018, before this Commission.



3.         The complainant has filed a complaint dated 07.09.2018 inter alia on the ground
that the CPIO and the FAA had intentionally denied the information. She alleged that
the respondent's act was against the 
RTI Act
.



4.         The CPIO vide letter dated 22.05.2018 gave point-wise reply to the RTI
application wherein on point nos. 1 to 9 of the RTI application, they stated that
information sought did not fall within the definition of information as defined under

section 2 (f)
 of the RTI Act. The FAA vide his order dated 16.07.2018 agreed with the
views taken by the CPIO and denied the information on point nos. 1 to 9 of the RTI
application in terms of 
section 2 (f)
 and 
8 (1) (e)
 of the RTI Act.



5.         The complainant's representatives, Shri Anil Singhal & Shri Aman Singhal and
on behalf of the respondent, Shri P.M. Meena, Asstt. General Manager & CPIO and
Shri Rahul Gupta, Legal Officer, Bank of Baroda, Bharatpur attended the hearing
through video conference.



                                                                                    Page 3 of 6


 5.1.   The representative of the complainant inter alia submitted that the complainant
is daughter and legal heir of the deceased partner late Shri Jagdish Prasad Agarwal in
firm M/s Jagdish Prasad Agarwal and Firm M/s Vijay automobiles which got dissolved
on his death on 08.08.2016 as per operation of law. He stated that a new partnership
deed dated 10.08.2016 with regard to M/s Jagdish Prasad which was formed by
remaining surviving partners after death of Jagdish Prasad Agarwal without following
legal procedures. Hence, being a legal heir of the deceased partner, the complainant
sought information related to the rules followed by the bank in respect of partnership
firm and related information. However, the respondent had denied the information on
one pretext or the other. The representative of the complainant alleges that the
respondent bank was actively involved in corrupt acts of surviving partners and not
closing the bank accounts of dissolved firm. He further requested the Commission to
take necessary action as per the provisions of the 
RTI Act
 for not providing the desired
information to the complainant.



5.2.   The respondent while defending their replies inter alia submitted that they had
already furnished point-wise reply to the RTI application vide letter dated 22.05.2018
within 9 days from the receipt of the application. They informed that the complainant
had filed various RTI application seeking a lot of information regarding the partnership
firm where his deceased father was one of the partners and all her RTI application had
been properly replied as per the provisions of the 
RTI Act
. They stated that it was not
their intention of conceal any information for which the complainant was entitled.
Further referring to the RTI application dated 13.05.2018, they stated that information
sought on point nos. 1 to 10 of the RTI application was in the form of queries, seeking
suggestions/ comments and opinion from the CPIO, hence, the same was not covered
under 
section 2 (f)
 of the RTI Act. They relied on the CIC order No.
CIC/AT/A/2006/00045 dated 21.04.2006. They stated that information sought on point
no. 10 was available in public domain and information sought point nos. 11 and 12 was


                                                                              Page 4 of 6

 denied on the ground of fiduciary relationship being exempted under 
section 8 (1) (e)
 of
the RTI Act.



6.    The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing both the parties and perusal of the records, observes that most of the queries
raised by the complainant in her RTI application was the form of seeking
explanations/opinion/suggestions which did not fall within the definition of information
as defined under 
section 2 (f)
 of the RTI Act. Further, the CPIO had furnished point-
wise reply to the RTI application vide letter dated 22.05.2018 and the FAA vide order
dated 16.07.2018. There was no delay in responding to the RTI application. Perusal of
the records reveals that the complainant has grievance with regard to her rights in a
partnership firm wherein her deceased father was one of the partners. She had filed
written complaints to Police authorities and CVO and also approached many forums
including Central Board of Direct Taxes (Income Tax) for redressal of her grievances.
In view of this and point-wise reply given by the CPIO, there appears to be no lapse on
the part of the CPIO, hence, no action need be initiated against the CPIO under 
section
20 (1)
 of RTI Act. Accordingly, the complaint is dismissed.
