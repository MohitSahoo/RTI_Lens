                                  ORDER



1.    The complainant filed an application under the 
Right to Information Act,
2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), O/o The
Assistant Commissioner Of Income Tax, Sagar seeking following information:-

      "1. Fate of enclosed letter dated 23-05-2018 issued by I.T.O. Ward I,
      Sagar.

      2. Certified true copy of intimation passed u/s 143(1)(a) for A.Y.
      2010-11 & 2011-12."

                                                                     Page 1 of 4


 2.     The CPIO responded on 12-02-2019 & 28-02-2019. Thereafter, the
complainant filed a complaint u/
Section 18
 of the RTI Act before the Commission
requesting to take appropriate legal action against the CPIO u/
Section 20
 of the
RTI Act, 2005.

Hearing:

3.     The complainant, Dr. Jayshree Chouksey vide letter dated 08-11-2020
informed the Commission that due to being affected by COVID-19, she is unable
to attend the hearing. Mr. Rakesh Kumar, CPIO and Mr. Anil Kumar Tiwari, CPIO
participated in the hearing representing the respondent(s) through video
conferencing. The written submissions dated 28-10-2020, 03-11-2020 & 09-11-
2020 are taken on record.

4.     Reiterating their written submissions, the respondent(s) submitted as
follows:-

        "PAN and case record of the assessee was transferred to the ACIT
        Circle-Sagar on 29-06-2018. Further, the case record for the AY
        2010-11 had been submitted before the Pr.-CIT-II, Jabalpur along
        with proposal of 
section 263
 of I.T. Act, 1961. Therefore, the
        assessee was suggested to gather the required information from the
        Pr.-CIT-II, Jabalpur vide reply dated 28-02-2019."

Decision:

5.     Since the complainant is not present to attend the hearing, this Commission
takes note of the documents annexed with the complaint wherein she has
contended that the then CPIO/Katni did not transfer the RTI application as per the

RTI Act, 2005
.

6.     This Commission observes that the then CPIO/Katni, Mr. Umesh Kumar
Pillai had asked the complainant to approach the office of the Pr.-CIT-II, Jabalpur
rather than transferring the RTI application to the concerned CPIO holding the
records. It is a very serious matter, as he had sent the reply without applying his
mind thereby causing inordinate delay in supplying the information. Such a
lackadaisical approach of Mr. Umesh Kumar Pillai is strongly deprecated and
therefore, he is hereby issued a very strict warning to be more vigilant in future and
not to contravene the provisions of the 
RTI Act, 2005
.

7.     This Commission further observes that while examining the complaint
under 
Section 18
 of the RTI Act, the CIC has no jurisdiction to direct disclosure of
any information. This legal position has been authoritatively settled by the
Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in 
Chief Information Commissioner and
Another v. State of Manipur and Anr.
 in Civil Appeal Nos. 10787-10788 of

                                                                            Page 2 of 4

 2011 dated 12-12-2011. The relevant extract of 
the said decision
 is set down
below:-


         "30. It has been contended before us by the respondent that
         under 
Section 18
 of the Act the Central Information
         Commission or the State Information Commission has no power
         to provide access to the information which has been requested
         for by any person but which has been denied to him. The only
         order which can be passed by the Central Information
         Commission or the State Information Commission, as the case
         may be, under 
Section 18
 is an order of penalty provided
         under 
Section 20
.

         However, before such order is passed the Commissioner must
         be satisfied that the conduct of the Information Officer was not
         bona fide.

         31. We uphold the said contention and do not find any error in
         the impugned judgment of the High court whereby it has been
         held that the Commissioner while entertaining a complaint
         under 
Section 18
 of the said Act has no jurisdiction to pass an
         order providing for access to the information."

8.   With the above observations, the complaint is disposed of.

9.   Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
