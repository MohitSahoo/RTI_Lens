Grounds for Second Appeal:

The Appellant filed a Second Appeal u/s 19 of the Act on the ground of non-
receipt of information from the Respondent. He requested the Commission to
direct the CPIO to provide complete information sought for.



Submissions made by Appellant and Respondent during Hearing:
The Appellant stated that he is not satisfied with the reply provided by the
Respondent. Upon queried by the Commission to demonstrate the element of his
dissatisfaction, he did not provide a satisfactory response. However, he asserted to
direct the Respondent to provide the relevant information as sought in the instant
RTI Application through post.



Shri Anil Kumar Khandelwal, PIO and Project-Director, PIU-Ajmer; Shri Harish
Chandra, Project Director-Chittorgarh; Shri Lokesh Singh Rajpurohit, Project
Director/GM-T, Udaipur reiterated the contents of their respective written
submissions. Upon queried by the Commission as to how voluminous is the
information sought, they explained that the information sought is not just
voluminous but scattered through various files in different offices. Hence, the
Appellant was asked to inspect the relevant records from the concerned offices,
which has not been availed till date.



The Appellant interjected to state that he cannot visit the Respondent's office until
and unless he is clearly informed with the fact that which document will be
allowed to inspect and at which Respondent office. He vehemently asserted that
the actions of the Respondents are nothing but a scam and smacks of corruption. At
the instance of the Commission to justify his statement, he yet again failed to
justify his statement.

Decision:

Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the proceedings
during the hearing, the Commission observes that the Appellant, in his feeble
arguments has not provided a tenable reason as to why the Commission should
direct the Respondent to furnish the information as sought in the instant RTI
Application through post. It is factually evident that the information sought is
voluminous in nature and scattered through various files in different offices; the
Appellant has not regarded the Respondent's offer of inspection till date.
In view of the above, the Commission is left with no other alternative to direct the
concerned CPIOs of PIU-Ajmer, PIU-Chittorgarh and PIU-Udaipur to provide an
opportunity to the Appellant to inspect relevant records on a mutually decided date
and time, duly intimated to the Appellant telephonically and in writing. Copy of
documents, if desired, will be provided free of cost upto 25 pages, and beyond
those, prescribed fees shall be charged as per the RTI Rules, 2012. The concerned
CPIOs are further directed that they shall ensure that any personal
information/identifying particulars of a third party should be adequately
redacted/blackened out. The said direction should be complied with, by the
concerned CPIOs within 3 months from the date of receipt of this order and a
compliance report of the same be duly sent to the Commission, enumerating the
details of documents inspected and copy of documents provided.
With the above observations, the instant Second Appeal is disposed of.
Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.

                     The Appeal, hereby, stands disposed of.
