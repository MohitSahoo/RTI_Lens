Relevant Facts emerging during Hearing:

The following were present:-



Appellant: Present through video conference.

Respondent: Braj Bhushan Singh, Law Officer & CPIO present through video

The Appellant stated that he has received the information on points 1 & 7 of the
RTI Application but for the remaining points he has been informed that the
information is not available even after the FAA's order. He expressed disbelief at
the claim of unavailability of any of the information sought for in the remaining
points, particularly points 2 & 3 of the RTI Application.



The CPIO submitted that the Appellant was informed on 17.07.2020 that no such
information is available based on the inputs received by the concerned section.



The Appellant requested that directions be ordered to provide the information as
is available with any other concerned section with reference to his own
appointment atleast.



Decision:



The Commission based on a perusal of the facts on record observes at the outset
that the information sought for at points 3-6, 8 & 9 of the RTI Application entails
disclosure of third-party related information, disclosure of which may cause
unwarranted invasion of the privacy of the third parties as per 
Section 8(1)(j)
 of
the RTI Act. 
In this regard, the attention of the Appellant is drawn towards a
judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court in the matter of Central Public
Information Officer, Supreme Court of 
India Vs. Subhash Chandra Agarwal
 in
Civil Appeal No. 10044 of 2010 with Civil Appeal No. 10045 of 2010 and Civil
Appeal No. 2683 of 2010 wherein the import of "personal information" envisaged
under 
Section 8(1)(j)
 of RTI Act has been exemplified in the context of earlier
ratios 
laid down by
 the same Court in the matter(
s) of Canara Bank Vs. C.S.
Shyam
 in Civil Appeal No.22 of 2009; 
Girish Ramchandra Deshpande vs. Central


                                        4

 Information Commissioner & Ors.
, (2013) 1 SCC 212 and R.K. Jain vs. Union of
India & Anr., (2013) 14 SCC 794. The following was thus held:




       "59. Reading of the aforesaid judicial precedents, in our opinion, would
indicate that personal records, including name, address, physical, mental and
psychological status, marks obtained, grades and answer sheets, are all treated as
personal information. Similarly, professional records, including qualification,
performance, evaluation reports, ACRs, disciplinary proceedings, etc. are all
personal information. Medical records, treatment, choice of medicine, list of
hospitals and doctors visited, findings recorded, including that of the family
members, information relating to assets, liabilities, income tax returns, details of
investments, lending and borrowing, etc. are personal information. Such personal
information is entitled to protection from unwarranted invasion of privacy and
conditional access is available when stipulation of larger public interest is
satisfied. This list is indicative and not exhaustive..."



Nonetheless, the Commission notes that the after the FAA's order, the reply
provided on 17.07.2020 merely mentions the unavailability of information with
the Department of Computer Science & Applications and not the University per
se. Infact, the CPIO has mentioned therein that the information may be available
with the Establishment Section but no further correspondence appears to have
been sent to the Appellant in this regard by the Respondent office.



Now, to allay the apprehensions of the Appellant and in keeping with the letter
and spirit of the 
RTI Act
, the CPIO is directed to adequately liaise with all the
concerned record holders of the University by seeking their assistance under

Section 5(4)
 of the RTI Act to ascertain the availability of information on point
no.2 of the RTI Application. Similarly, on points 3-6 of the RTI Application, the
availability of the information limited to the Appellant's appointment shall be
ascertained by the CPIO and be provided based on the availability of records by
severing the third-party related information as per 
Section 10
 of the RTI Act. The
available information on point no.2 of the RTI Application and on points 3-6 of the
RTI Application, after severing the third-party information shall be provided free
of cost to the Appellant by the CPIO.



In the event that no such information is available with any of the concerned
record holders in the University, the CPIO will file an appropriate affidavit stating
to this effect inter alia that after liaising with the concerned sections, no

                                         5

 relevant records as sought for at points 2 and 3-6 of the RTI Application is
available with the University.



The aforesaid directions shall be complied with by the CPIO within 30 days from
the date of receipt of this order under due intimation to the Commission.



                    The appeal is disposed of accordingly.
