                                  ORDER

1.     The appellant filed an application under the 
Right to Information Act,
2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO) O/o. The
Income Tax Officer, Ward No. 3(3), Jaipur. The appellant seeking information
is as under:-

       1.     Please advise date of allotment of this PAN No. ADOPA9202D

       2.     Pleas provide certified copy of above PAN Card.

       3.     Please provide certified copy of all annual income Tax returns
              from the dated of allotment of above PAN No. ADOPA9202D to
              AY 2019-20

       4.     Name, Designations, Office Address, Office Telephone Number,
              Mobile Numbers (in case mobile provided and paid for by the
              office) of all the officers of the income tax department who shall
              deal with this matter.



                                                                       Page 1 of 5


       5.    The name, office address, office telephone number and mobile
            number of the higher officer to whom a first appeal will lie under
            sec 19((1) of the 
RTI Act, 2005
.



2.    The CPIO vide letter dated 07-01-2020 had denied the information sought
by the applicant under 
section 8(1)(j)
 of the RTI Act, 2005.Being dissatisfied
with the same, the appellant has filed first appeal dated 16-01-2020and
requested that the information should be provided to him. The FAA vide order
dated 07-02-2020 upheld CPIO's reply and disposed the appeal. He has filed a
second appeal before the Commission on the ground that information sought
has not been provided to him and requested to direct the respondent to provide
complete and correct information.



Hearing:



3.     The appellant attended the hearing through audio-call. The respondent,
Shri Hardayal Singh Rathore, CPIO/ITO attended the hearing through audio-
call.

4.     The respondent submitted their written submissions dated 01.11.2021 and
the same has been taken on record.

5.     The appellant reiterated the contents of his RTI Application and
submitted that the desired information has not been provided to him by the
respondent on his RTI application dated 10.12.2019.He further submitted that
he is not the sole successor of the deceased. He has relied on few judgments of
the Commission bearing file nos. 1) CIC/POSTS/A/2017/131704 titled as 
S
Sankaralingam vs Department Of Posts
, 2) CIC/POSTS/C/2017/139615 titled
as Premlata Suri Vs PIO, Dept of Post and 3) CIC/SBIND/A/2017/180954 titled
as 
Saroj Devi Vs CPIO, State Bank of India
, wherein the Commission has
ordered to provide the requested information to the appellant.


6.     The respondent submitted that vide their letter dated 07.01.2020, they
have informed the appellant that the information sought by him is exempted
under 
section 8 (1) (j)
 of the RTI Act, 2005. Hence, the information sought
cannot be provided to the appellant. That thereafter the respondent in
compliance to the direction of the concerned FAA, has issued a letter dated
12.02.2020 to the appellant to furnish details regarding copy of legal heir
certificate and other supported documents on 20.02.2020. That on 19.02.2020
the applicant Sh. Ashish Bansal has furnished copy of adhar and death
certificate but the applicant has not furnished copy of legal heir certificate.



                                                                      Page 2 of 5


 Therefore, his application was rejected. That the disclosure of such information
has no relationship with larger public interest also.



Decision:



7.     The Commission, after hearing the submissions of both the parties and
after perusal of records, observes that the appellant has sought certified copy of
ITR for A.Y 2019-20, copy of PAN Card and details of date of allotment of
PAN in the case of Late Smt. Chalti Devi Agarwal (PAN:- ADOPA9202D) and
other related queries. That the appellant has referred few judgments passed by
the     Commission      bearing     nos.1)     CIC/POSTS/A/2017/131704,          2)
CIC/POSTS/C/2017/139615 and 3) CIC/SBIND/A/2017/180954in support of
his arguments which are not relevant here. That the Commission has gone
through these judgments carefully and observed that in file no.

CIC/POSTS/A/2017/131704, the appellant has been provided the relevant
information being the legitimate heir of the deceased and in file no.
CIC/POSTS/C/2017/139615, the appellant has been provided the relevant
information as the appellant was declared as nominee of the deceased and in file
no. CIC/SBIND/A/2017/180954, the Commission has specifically directed the
concerned public authority to provide the relevant information only after
receiving the document certifying that the appellant is the legal heir of the
deceased. Therefore, none of the judgments referred by the appellant is relevant
in the present case as neither the appellant is the sole legal heir of the deceased
nor he has furnished the copy of legal heir certificate.

8.     The Commission observes that since the appellant is seeking third party
personal information and such information is held under a fiduciary capacity,
therefore the same cannot be provided under 
Section 8 (1) (e)
 & 
section 8 (1)


(j) of the 
RTI Act
. The Commission also examined the nature of fiduciary
relationship involved in the instant matter whereby the details of date of
allotment of PAN of Late Smt. Chalti Devi Agarwalwere sought which is
exempted from disclosure under 
Section 8(1)(e)
 of the RTI Act, 2005. The
Commission referred to the Hon'ble Supreme Court decision in 
CBSE v.
Aditya Bandhopadhyay
 (2011) 8 SCC 497 wherein it was held as under:


       22. ".... But the words 'information available to a person in his fiduciary
       relationship' are used in 
section 8(1)(e)
 of RTI Act in its normal and well
       recognized sense, that is to refer to persons who act in a fiduciary
       capacity, with reference to a specific beneficiary or beneficiaries who are




                                                                         Page 3 of 5

       to be expected to be protected or benefited by the actions of the fiduciary

      - a trustee with reference to the beneficiary of the trust, a guardian with
      reference to a minor/physically/infirm/mentally challenged, a parent with
      reference to a child, a lawyer or a chartered accountant with reference to
      a client, a doctor or nurse with reference to a patient, an agent with
      reference to a principal, a partner with reference to another partner, a
      director of a company with reference to a share-holder, an executor with
      reference to a legatee, a receiver with reference to the parties to a lis, an
      employer with reference to the confidential information relating to the
      employee, and an employee with reference to business
      dealings/transaction of the employer."



9.     Moreover, the Commission observes that the appellant has failed to
establish larger public interest in seeking such information; therefore the
respondent has rightly denied the information sought under the relevant
provision of the 
RTI Act
.

10. 
In this regard, the Commission referred to the judgment of the Hon'ble
Supreme Court of India in the matter of 
GirishRamchandra Deshpande vs.
Central Information Commission
 & ors. SLP(C) No. 27734 of 2012 dated
03/10/2012 wherein it was held as under:


       "13......The performance of an employee/officer in an organization is
       primarily a matter between the employee and the employer and normally
       those aspects are governed by the service rules which fall under the
       expression "personal information", the disclosure of which has no
       relationship to any public activity or public interest. On the other hand,
       the disclosure of which would cause unwarranted invasion of privacy of
       that individual. Of course, in a given case, if the Central Public
       Information Officer or the State Public Information Officer of the
       Appellate Authority is satisfied that the larger public interest justifies the
       disclosure of such information, appropriate orders could be passed but
       the petitioner cannot claim those details as a matter of right."



      14. The details disclosed by a person in his income tax returns are
      "personal information" which stand exempted from disclosure under
      clause(j) of 
Section 8(1)
 of the RTI Act, unless involves a larger public
      interest and the Central Public Information Officer or the State Public
      Information Officer or the Appellate Authority is satisfied that the larger
      public interest justifies the disclosure of such information.



                                                                           Page 4 of 5


 11. In view of the above ratios, the Commission is of the opinion that the
CPIO has rightly denied the information sought under 
Section 8 (1) (e)
 &

Section 8 (1) (j)
 of the RTI Act and same is being upheld by the Commission.
Hence, no further intervention of the Commission is required in the matter.

12. With the above observations, the appeal is disposed of.

13. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
