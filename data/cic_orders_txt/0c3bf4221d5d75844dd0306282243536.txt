Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:

In order to ensure social distancing and prevent the spread of the pandemic,
COVID-19, video hearing has been scheduled after giving prior notice to both the
parties. Appellant is not present for hearing through video conference while
Respondent is duly represented and averred that on deposit of the requisite fees
by the Appellant, information sought by him in the appeal number
CIC/MHOME/A/2019/102639, had been provided. In so far as the First Appeal
is concerned, it was disposed off by the Additional Secretary, UT. He further
averred that the Appellant has retired now and the investigation pending against
the appellant is about to reach finality within a month. Respondent is allowed
time to submit a complete status report, during the course of the day.

 Decision:

 Upon perusal of the records of the case, it is noted that the queries raised by the
 Appellant had been responded from time to time, as mandated under the
 provisions of the 
RTI Act

 cases.



 Under the circumstances, no further adjudication is deemed necessary in these
 cases.



 The appeals are disposed off as such.
