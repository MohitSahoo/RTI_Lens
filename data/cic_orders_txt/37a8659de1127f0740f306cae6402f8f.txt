1.      The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated
28.08.2018 include non-receipt of the following information raised by the
appellant through his RTI application dated 3.13.2018 and first appeal dated
02.06.2018:-

        The period/periods of deputation of Sh. V. N. Jaitly, served/serving in the
        Department of Inspection and Audit at Punjab National Bank, Head Office,

                                                                          Page 1 of 4

        Delhi, to Haryana Kshetriya Gramin Bank, Head Office, Bhiwani (Haryana)
       now known as Sarva Haryana Gramin Bank, having its Head Office at
       SHGB House, Plot No. 1, Sector - 3, Rohtak - 124001 (Haryana).

2.     Succinctly facts of the case are that the Appellant filed an application dated
13.03.2018 under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the Central
Public Information Officer (CPIO), Punjab National Bank, Head Office, Bikaji
Cama Place, New Delhi, seeking aforesaid information. The CPIO vide letter dated
25.04.2018 replied to the RTI application. Dissatisfied with the response of the
CPIO, the Appellant filed First Appeal dated 02.06.2018. The FAA vide order
dated disposed of the first appeal vide order dated 05.07.2018. Aggrieved by this,
the Appellant has filed a Second Appeal dated 28.08.2018 before this Commission
which is under consideration.

3.     The appellant filed the instant appeal dated 28.08.2018 inter alia on the
grounds that the reply given by the CPIO was not satisfactory. The appellant has
requested the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the information
immediately and take necessary action as per sub-section (1) of 
section 20
 of the
RTI Act.

4.     The CPIO vide letter dated 25.04.2018 replied that desired information
could not be provided as the appellant had not mentioned employee number/PF
No. of Shri V.N. Jaitley. The FAA vide his order dated 05.07.2018 stated that they
did not find any employee working/retired in recent past from their organization
with name of Shri V.N. Jaitley or Vishwa Nath Jaitley.

5.     The appellant attended the hearing through video conference and on behalf
of the respondent, Shri Ranjit Singh, Chief Manager, Punjab National Bank, Delhi
attended the hearing in person.

5.1.   The appellant inter alia submitted that he sought information regarding
deputation period of Sh. V. N. Jaitley (Vishwa Nath Jaitley) served/serving in the
department of Inspection and Audit at Punjab National Bank, Head Office, Delhi

                                                                           Page 2 of 4

 and who was deputed to Haryana Kshetriya Gramin Bank, Head Office Bhiwani
(Haryana) now known as Sarva Haryana Gramin Bank, having its Head Offlce at
SHGB House, Rotak-l, Haryana. He further submitted that Shri Jaitley served
Haryana Kshetriya Gramin Bank, Head Office Bhiwani sometime in 1991 to
1996. He also stated that a departmental enquiry was conducted against him during
that period wherein Mr. V.N. Jaitley was the enquiry officer. He informed that he
needed this information just to prove in the court of law that appointment of Shri
Jaitley as an enquiry office was against the extant rules.
5.2. The respondent while defending their replies inter alia submitted that they did
not find any employee working/retired in recent past from their organization
having name of Shri V.N. Jaitley. They further submitted that in the absence of
further particulars of Shri Jaitley, they could not find any records on their database.

6.    The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing both parties and perusal of records revealed that the respondent could not
provide the information on the plea that the specific details including PF number
were not provided by the applicant while seeking the information. During the
course of hearing, the appellant further submitted that he was not interested in
personal details of Mr. V.N.Jaitley, he wanted only the period of deputation which
was somewhere between 1991 to 1996. The appellant further submitted that a
departmental enquiry was conducted against him wherein Mr. V.N. Jaitley was the
enquiry officer. He was of the view that departmental enquiry was conducted in
contravention of the extant rules. Therefore, he wanted the information about the
deputation period of Mr. V.N.Jaitley in the Haryana Gramin Bank (now Sarva
Haryana Gramin Bank). It was not the case that the respondent had claimed any
exemption, however, they had expressed their inability to lay hands on the records
showing Mr. V.N.Jaitley as their employee for want of details like EPF number
etc. The Commission is of the view that the respondent should give unambiguous
reply either he was not on deputation or provide the period of deputation. If they

                                                                              Page 3 of 4

 feel they may seek copy of the enquiry report from the appellant. If they are not
able to provide the information for any reason, they may say so on affidavit within
four weeks from the date of receipt of the order. With the above observations and
directions, the appeal is disposed of.
