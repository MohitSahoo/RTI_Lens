2.    Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
01.10.2018 under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the Central Public
Information Officer (CPIO), Reserve Bank of India, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, seeking
aforesaid information. The CPIO, RBI, vide letter dated 22.10.2018 transferred the RTI
application to the CPIO, Mudra Limited, MSME Development Centre, Bandra Kurla
Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai and the CPIO, Bank of Baroda, Baroda Corporate
Centre, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai, under 
section 6 (3)
 of the RTI Act
for providing the proper reply/information available with them. Dissatisfied with the
response of the CPIO, the appellant filed first appeal dated 01.11.2018. Subsequently, the
CPIO, RBI, Mudra, Mumbai, replied on05.11.2018& 26.11.2018. The First Appellate
Authority (FAA), RBI, Mumbai, did not pass any order within the stipulated time period.
Aggrieved by this, the appellant filed a second appeal dated 26.11.2018 before this
Commission which is under consideration.

3.       The appellant has filed the instant appeal dated 09.11.2018 inter alia on the
grounds that desired information was not provided respondent. The appellant requested
the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide complete information free of cost as per

section 7 (6)
 of the RTI Act and impose penalty on the concerned CPIO @ 250/- per day
as per 
Section 20 (1)
& (2) of the 
RTI Act
.



4.    The CPIO, RBI, vide letter dated 22.10.2018 transferred the RTI application to the
CPIO, Mudra Limited, MSME Development Centre, Bandra Kurla Complex, Bandra
East, Mumbai and the CPIO, Bank of Baroda, Baroda Corporate Centre, Bandra Kurla
Complex, Bandra East, Mumbai, under 
section 6 (3)
 of the RTI Act for providing the
proper reply/information available with them. The CPIO, RBI, Mudra Limited, Mumbai
vide letter dated 05.11.2018 provided information on point nos. 7 & 8 of the RTI
application and regarding remaining points stated that information sought was not
available with them. Subsequently, the CPIO, RBI, Mudra Limited, Mumbai vide letter


                                                                                 Page 4 of 7

 dated 26.11.2018 provided revised reply on point nos. 9 and 10 of the RTI application.
The FAA did not pass any order within the stipulated time.

5.     The appellant and on behalf of the respondent Shri Ashok J. Parikh, General
Manager and CPIO, Reserve Bank of India, HRD, Mumbai (respondent no. 1), Shri
Aditya Mishra, CPIO and Assistant General Manager, MUDRA Limited, Mumbai
(respondent no. 2) and Shri D C Khanna, PIO, Bank of Baroda, Mumbai (respondent
no.3)attended the hearing through video conference.



5.1.   The appellant inter alia submitted that he was not satisfied with the replies given
by the then CPIO of RBI, Mudra, as the same was incomplete and evasive. He further
submitted that the CPIO of Bank of Baroda to whom the RTI application was forwarded
did not respond      till the date of hearing. Therefore, the appellant requested the
Commission to direct the respondent to provide complete information sought by him in
his RTI application without further delay.



5.2.   The respondent no. 1 inter alia submitted that they had transferred the RTI
application to the CPIO, Mudra Limited, MSME Development Centre, Bandra East,
Mumbai and the CPIO, Bank of Baroda, Baroda Corporate Centre, Bandra East, Mumbai,
under 
section 6 (3)
 of the RTI Act for providing the proper reply/information directly to
the appellant as per their record. Thereafter, the respondent no. 2 (CPIO, RBI, Mudra
Limited, Mumbai) submitted that they vide letter dated 05.11.2018 and 26.11.2018 had
already provided information against point nos. 7, 8, 9 and 10 of the RTI application and
remaining points, they informed that information sought was not available with them.
Further, they informed that the first appeal of the appellant was also disposed of by the
FAA, RBI, Mumbai, vide its order dated 05.12.2018.The respondent no. 3 (BOB) inter
alia informed that after receiving the hearing notice of the Commission they had provided
a revised point-wise information/reply to the appellant vide their letter dated 05.01.2020.
They also tendered unconditional apology for the inconvenience caused to the appellant
as well as the Commission.



6.     The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
both the parties and perusal of records, observes that due reply has been given by the

                                                                                 Page 5 of 7

 respondents vide their letters dated 05.11.2018, 26.11.2018 and 05.01.2020. Although,
the respondent no. 3 has given delayed reply, however, the same appears               to be
inadvertent. Besides, some of the queries of the RTI application was non-specific and
definite, and the same did not squarely fall within the definition of "information" as
defined under 
section 2 (f)
 of the RTI Act.That being so and reply/information having
been given to the appellant, there appear to be no public interest in further prolonging the
matter. Accordingly, the appeal is dismissed.
