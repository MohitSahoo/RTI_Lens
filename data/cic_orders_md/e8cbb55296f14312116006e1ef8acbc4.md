## Information sought

## and background of the case:


The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 19.04.2018 seeking information on
## the following points:


    1. Has the RWA of H Block DDA SFS Flats, Saket, New Delhi 110017,approached
       your office for permission for erection of iron gates at motorable entry and exit
       namely gate no.1, gate no.2, gate no.3 and gate no.4.

    2. Has any permission been granted to the RWA of H Block DDA SFS Flats by your
       office for installation of iron gates at motorable entry and exit namely gate no. 1,
       gate no.2, gate no.3 and gate no.4 vide policy guidelines for installation of gates
       for security purpose D/664/SE(P)I dated 25/6/07.

    3. If permission is granted by your office is the RWA of H Block DDA SFS Flats,
       Saket adhering to the points laid down vide policy guidelines for installation of
       gates for security purpose D/664/SE(P)I dated 25/6/07.

    4. If no permission is issued by your office what action has been taken by SDMC to
       remove the iron gates on motorable entry and exit namely gate no. 1, gate no. 2,
       gate no. 3 and gate no. 4.



                                                                  (Queries are Verbatim)

 The PIO/EE-M-IV-SZ, Delhi informed the Appellant vide his reply dated
07.05.2018 on point no.1 of the RTI Application that no information was
available in their division; on point nos.2 to 4 of the RTI Application it was
informed that since the gate was erected long back, no information was
available in their office.



Dissatisfied with the reply received from the PIO, the Appellant filed First
## Appeal dated 11.05.2018. The FAA vide order dated 28.05.2018 directed the

PIO/EE(M)IV/SZ to provide a revised and specific information to the Appellant
within ten days therefrom as per the provisions of the 
RTI Act
.



Feeling aggrieved over non-compliance of FAO, Appellant approached the
Commission with the instant Second Appeal.



## Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:


A communication has been received from the Appellant vide letter dated
22.05.2020 wherein he has annexed a copy of the decision of a predecessor
Bench vide File No. CIC/DEPOL/A/2017/149010 dated 14.12.2017 in the
matter of Mr. Satbir Singh v/s. CPIO, O/o. the Addl. Dy. Commissioner of
Police, South Delhi and a copy of the order of Hon'ble High Court of Delhi
dated 06.02.2004 in the matter of G.K. Bajaj v/s. State (Govt. of NCT of
Delhi) and others vide Criminal Revision Petition No. 177 of 2003.



In order to ensure social distancing and prevent the spread of the pandemic,
COVID-19, audio hearings were scheduled after giving prior notice to both the
parties.



The Appellant participated in the hearing on being contacted on his telephone
number and stated that the Respondent public authority has not complied with
the order of the First Appellate Authority order till date. He also relied upon two
## orders mentioned in his written submissions and requested the Commission to

take them on record.



Respondent is represented by Shri Bhushan Kumar, Ex. Engineer/PIO through
audio conference. He regretted that since he is not present in the office, he may
not be in a position to assist in the proceedings of the hearing.



## Decision

Upon perusal of the facts on record, Commission observes that the reliance
placed by the Appellant on a decision of a predecessor Bench File No.
CIC/DEPOL/A/2017/149010 dated 14.12.2017 in the matter of Mr. Satbir
Singh v/s. CPIO, O/o. the Addl. Dy. Commissioner of Police, South Delhi,
## wherein the Commission held as under:




      "Both parties are present and heard. The appellant stated that a letter
      was submitted to SHO Saket by Federation of RWA, Saket on 01.09.2016


                                                                          Page 2 of 4

       intimating that the road in from of J-1 to 20, parallel to M.B.Road Saket
      has been permanently blocked by the residents by installing and
      permanently locking the gate opposite J-19-20 Saket and also requested
      for implementation of Delhi High Court Order dated 06.02.2004 (Crl. Rev.
      Petition No. 177/2003 and Cr.(MA) No. 134/2003. He further added that
      information furnished in this regard is false and misleading. The
      respondent states that information as available has already been
      furnished to the appellant. Regarding the gate, it was locked long back
      and the situation will be assessed afresh in the light of latest position.



## Decision:


      Both parties are present and heard. The Commission finds that the
      respondents have provided an evasive reply to a precise query. The
      appellant has demanded the opening of the said gate in the light of the
      High Court order as quoted by him, which directs that no gates can be
      permanently closed but the ingress/egress be maintained manually.
      Despite the FAA hearing, no proper information has been provided. Since
      the respondent have stated that the matter will be looked into within two
      weeks and appropriate decision taken, the Commission directs the
      respondents to take action and intimate the action taken report to the
      appellant under intimation to the Commission within 2 weeks of the receipt
      of this order."



Further, the Appellant has also relied upon an order of Hon'ble High Court of
Delhi dated 06.02.2004 in the matter of G.K. Bajaj v/s. State (Govt. of NCT
of Delhi) and others vide Criminal Revision Petition No. 177 of 2003,
## wherein the Hon'ble High Court has held as under:




      "Adjourned to 23rd July, 2004.



      In the meantime, it is directed that all the barriers and gates put up by any
      person, in the entire colony of Saket, on public land shall be kept open. In
      the event any of the barriers or gates are sought to be closed at night that
      barrier or gate must be manned permitting free egress and ingress to
      anybody who wants to go through it. It is also made clear that any wall
      constructed by any individual on public land will also be demolished.



      This order shall be promptly complied with. The Chowki-in-charge, Saket is
      directed to ensure the compliance of this order."



Upon perusal of the aforesaid orders relied by the Appellant, Commission is not
in a position to comment since decisions are given on a case-by-case basis.
Moreover, it is apparent that the order of the First Appellate Authority has not
been complied with till date.



                                                                          Page 3 of 4


 In view of the foregoing, Commission directs the PIO to submit an explanation
as to why the order of the First Appellate Authority has not been complied with
till date. The explanation shall reach the Commission on or before 30.06.2020.



In addition, PIO is also directed to provide a detailed action taken report with
respect to the information sought in the instant RTI Application to the
Appellant and a compliance report shall be submitted to the Commission
within 4 weeks from the date of issue of this order.



With the aforesaid directions, the instant Second Appeal is disposed off.
