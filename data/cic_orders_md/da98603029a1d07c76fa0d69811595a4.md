## Hearing:




7.     The appellant was personally present in the hearing. The respondent, Shri
Vinod Kumar, DGM & CPIO attended the hearing through video-conferencing.

The respondent, Shri Deepak Chikhale, CPIO attended the hearing through
video-conferencing.

8.     The written submissions of the parties are taken on record.

9.     Both the parties are agreed for adjudication of above cases together.

10. The appellant submitted that desired information has not been provided to
him by the respondents on his above mentioned RTI application dated
19.12.2019, 27.12.2019 & 21.01.2020.

11. The respondent w.r.t file no. CIC/RBIND/A/2020/665832 submitted that
vide their letter dated 10.01.2020, point-wise reply/information has been
provided to the appellant on his RTI application. The respondent submitted that
the factual position in the matter as on the date of the RTI application has been
provided     to    the     appellant.    The     respondent    w.r.t    file  no.
CIC/RBIND/A/2020/667428 submitted that vide their letter dated 24.01.2020,
they have categorically informed the appellant that the information sought by
him is exempted from disclosure under 
Section 8(1)(h)
 of the RTI Act. The
respondent w.r.t file no. CIC/RBIND/A/2020/672796 submitted that vide their

informing him the factual position in the matter. The replies were seen during
the hearing.



## Decision:


12. The Commission, after hearing the submissions of both the parties and
after perusal of records, observes that the appellant was aggrieved that complete
and correct information has not been provided to him by the respondent on his
abovementioned RTI applications. The appellant contended that despite the
## decision in Jayantilal Mistry case no information was provided to him in file no.

CIC/RBIND/A/2020/665832. The appellant further contended that the
respondent has wrongly invoked exemption clause of 
RTI Act
 in denying the
information. The Commission observed that the respondent in file no.
CIC/RBIND/A/2020/665832 has informed the factual position in the matter to
the appellant as on the date of reply of the RTI application and the decision of
Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in Jayantilal Mistry case had passed
afterwards. Further the respondent has rightly invoked 
Section 8(1)(h)
 of the
RTI Act in file no. CIC/RBIND/A/2020/667428. The Commission further


                                                                        Page 4 of 6

 observed in file no. CIC/RBIND/A/2020/672796 that some queries of the
appellant are more in the nature of seeking explanation/opinion/advice from the
CPIO and has also raised situational queries viz. 'When the first circular was
issued by RBI for the requirement of KYC for saving bank accounts & provide
the copy of this circular', etc. and he has expected that the CPIO firstly should
analyze the documents and then provide information to the appellant. But the
CPIO is not supposed to create information; or to interpret information; or to
furnish clarification to the appellant under the ambit of the 
RTI Act
. As per

Section 2(f)
 of the RTI Act, the reasons/opinions/advices can only be provided
to the applicants if it is available on record of the public authority. The CPIO
cannot create information in the manner as sought by the appellant. The CPIO is
only a communicator of information based on the records held in the office and
hence, he cannot expected to do research work to deduce anything from the
material therein and then supply it to him.

13. Nonetheless, the respondent has given point-wise reply/information to the
appellant on his abovementioned RTI applications. The Commission is satisfied
with the action/steps taken by the respondent in dealing with the RTI
applications of the appellant. No further intervention of the Commission is
required in the matter.

14. With the above observations, the appeals are disposed of.

15. Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.
