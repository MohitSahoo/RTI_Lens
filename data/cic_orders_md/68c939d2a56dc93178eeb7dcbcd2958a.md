## ORDER




## Information sought:




The appellant filed an application under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005


(RTI Act
) before the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), Western
Railway, Mumbai seeking xerox copy of working chart of train no. 22946 for
date 01.04.2018 whereby the appellant travelled from Viramgam to Mumbai on
the said date. The appellant also sought information regarding whether the seat
allotted to her was confirmed sleeper seat or R.A.C seat.
The CPIO, vide letter dated 05.06.2018, provided information to the appellant.
The appellant filed a first appeal dated 12.06.2018 on the ground that the reply
furnished by CPIO is unsatisfactory. The FAA vide order dated 23.08.2018
disposed of the first appeal with the remarks that statutory fees chargeable
cannot be overridden by the RTI provision. Thereafter, the appellant filed a
second appeal
 u/s. 19(3)
 of the RTI Act before the Commission on similar
grounds and requested the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide the
## information sought for.




## Hearing on 29.04.2020:




The appellant submitted that due to the lockdown declared by the government in
view of Covid-19, he is not in possession of the complete file of his case. He
requested the Commission to adjourn the matter and fix another date of hearing.
Considering the request of the appellant the case was adjourned to 16.07.2020.



Submissions made by the Appellant and the Respondent during Hearing on
## 16.07.2020:




The Appellant submitted that the CPIO vide their reply dated 05.06.2018 has
demanded fees of Rs. 750/- per INR of all the PNRs indicated in the reservation
chart of train no. 22946 as per circular no. 65 of 2011. The appellant contended
that with the enactment of the 
RTI Act
, the respondent cannot demand more

than Rs.2/- per page of photocopy. At the end the appellant requested that the
CPIO be directed to provide the information as sought in the RTI application by
charging fee of Rs. 2/- per page as per the 
RTI Act
.

Per contra, the respondent has submitted that the FAA has rightly given the
## order for charging of fees as per the statutory circular no. 65 of 2011. Since the

information demanded by the appellant through her RTI is in the material form
of chart, so she is liable to pay fee of Rs. 750/- per PNR as per the circular no.
65 of 2011. The order of FAA is supported by this Commission's order dated
29.07.2016 CIC/VS/A/2014/001491, wherein it was held that the statutory fees
prescribed by the public authority cannot be overridden by the RTI provision.

The respondent contended that the reservation charts can be preserved for 6
months only. The reservation charts as sought by the appellant is of the year
2018 and the same has been weeded out as per the record retention schedule.
Hence, the respondent is unable to provide information in material.
## Decision:


The Commission after hearing the submissions of both the parties and perusal of
record observes that Hon'ble CIC in the matter of Kumar Saurabh v. PIO and
DGM (Law), Head Quarter Office, Northern Railway and Another
(CIC/NRAIL/A/2017/182950) decided on 17.10.2017 held as under -



   "......On conjoint reading of the above orders, it was observed that the
   Supreme Court in the SLP petition filed by Maharaja Ganga Singh
   University against the decision of the Rajasthan High Court was
   dismissed in limine by the Apex Court. Regarding the decision of the
   Hon'ble Delhi High Court in the case of Paras Jain, the Supreme Court
   has stayed the matter and it is pending for final decision of the Hon'ble
   court. Therefore, the Commission seeks to arrive at the conclusion that as
   the Hon'ble Supreme Court has not yet decided on the matter of
   applicability of administrative rule vis-a-vis the rule prescribed under
   rule 4 of the RTI Rules, 2012 in regard to the fixation of copying charge
   for supply of information held by the public authority concerned under



                                                                        Page 3 of 7

                                                    CIC/WRAIL/A/2018/152380

   the provisions of the 
RTI Act
, the existing RTI Rule shall prevail in the
   present case.



   It is also relevant to note that the Commission had earlier in similar
   subject matters not interfered with the circular of the railway board as it
   was not brought to the knowledge of the Commission that the Hon'ble
   Supreme Court had summarily dismissed the prayer by the respondent
   against the decision of the Rajasthan HC in the Maharaja Ganga Singh
   University case, in which the relevant RTI Rules was upheld overriding
   the University rules for providing photocopy of the documents sought for
   under the 
RTI Act


   In view of the above ratio, the respondent authority is directed to provide
   the sought for information as available on record by claiming
   photocopying charges as per the RTI Rules 2012 and as applicable
 u/s
   2(f)
 of the RTI Act within 15 days from the receipt of this order."



In view of the above ratio, demand of Rs. 750/- per INR as per the circular no.
65 of 2011 by the respondent was not justified. The Commission, considering
the statement of respondent that the reservation charts of 2018 has already been
weeded out as per the record retention schedule, found that no information can
be provided to the appellant at this stage.



The Commission further observes that there is negligence on the part of the
respondent, which is clear from the fact that despite pendency of an appeal on
appellant's RTI application, the respondent had destroyed the record of the
reservation chart of 2018 of train no. 22946. The Commission, therefore, directs
the Registry of this Bench to issue a Show Cause Notice to the CPIO, Western
Railway, Mumbai for explaining as to why action under 
Section 20(1)
 of the
RTI Act should not be initiated against him.



   With the aforesaid observation appeal stands disposed of.



   Copy of the decision be provided free of cost to the parties.



                The appeal stands disposed of accordingly.

                 ADJUNCT DECISION DATED 22.02.2021

In response to the Commission's Show-Cause notice/order dated 16.07.2020,
Shri Abhay Sanap, PIO and Divisional Commercial Manager (Sub), Mumbai
Central vide his explanation dated 25.08.2020, stated as under:

      "Smt. Vasanti P Kothari (Applicant) sought the working chart of Train No
      22946 Saurashtra Mail dated 01.04.2018 vide RTI application registration No
      WRLMD/R/2018/80467 dated 30.05.2018.



      This office vide Letter No C358/RTIA/WRLMD/R/2018/80467 dated
      05.06.2018 informed applicant about Railway Board's policy that information
      can be provided subject to Rs. 750/- per PNR as it is done in all the cases,
      including RTI Cases.



      The applicants vide her Letter dated 12.06.2018; (1st Appeal) refused to pay as
      per Railway Board's rules & insisted that she will pay only Rs-2/- page.



      Appellate Authority & ADRM (Non-Suburban) replied applicant vide Letter No
      C/RTIA/WRLMD/Appeal/2018/00163/2018-19 dated 23.08.2018 in reference
      to CIC's judgement Letter No CIC/VS/A/2014/001491/BJ dated 29.07.2016,
      that applicant has to pay charges as per Railway Rules.



      Thereafter, the applicant didn't respond or made any communication or even
      contacted this office about the said case.



      As per RTI ACTs 2005 if the applicant is not satisfy with the reply, he can file
      1st Appeal within 30 days & if applicant is not satisfy with the reply of
      Appellant Authority against 1st appeal he can file 2nd appeal within 90 days
      with CIC. Applicants time period of 90 days expired on 23.11.2018. as such it
      was deemed that the applicant does not want to proceed further.



      This office is in receipt of policy guidelines from Ministry of Railway dated
      02.08.1994 regarding retention of old records as Railway stations & premises.
      As per the record retention policy TTE's train working chart is preserved up to
      6 months and reservation chart up to 3 years.



      As such all the old records were destroyed/removed from the station premises
      as per the certificate issued by CTI office, Mumbai Central (custodian of
      records)

      The applicant made 2nd appeal directly to CIC on 24.08.2018, but no
      communication or information or intimation was given to the office. This office
      came to know of the 2nd Appeal only on 24.04.2020 as per CIC's notice
      No.CIC/WRAIL/A/2018/152380 dated 03.04.2020

      this office has a mechanism of retention of reservation chart in computer
      system. This chart gives information of Passenger Booked in a particular
      train/date/class etc. This office can provide the Copy of Reservation chart to
      the applicant.



      In view of above facts, I hereby request you to condone show Cause Notice

## Decision:


Upon perusal of the facts on record as well as on the basis of the written
explanation submitted by Shri Abhay Sanap, PIO and Divisional Commercial
Manager (Sub), Mumbai Central, the Commission is convinced/satisfied with
the explanation provided by Shri Abhay Sanap. Hence, the Show Cause
proceedings are hereby dropped.
