## Information sought

## and background of the case:


The Appellant filed online RTI application dated 26.02.2019 seeking information
pertaining to the marks obtained in Part A and Part B by all the successful
candidates pertaining to the post of Special Educator, Post Code 15/17, results
whereof had been declared on 25.02.2019.



Having not received any reply from the PIO, the Appellant filed an online First
## Appeal dated 04.04.2019 before the Dte. of Education. The FAA (HQ)/RDE

(North/Central) vide his order dated 29.04.2019 informed the appellant that his
 RTI Application and First Appeal has already been transferred to DSSSB,
therefore the appellant is advised to get information from DSSSB.



Feeling aggrieved and dissatisfied, the Appellant approached the Commission
with the instant Second Appeal.



## Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:


A written submission dated 20.05.2019 has been received from the appellant
reiterating facts of the instant case, which have already been discussed above.



In order to ensure social distancing and prevent the spread of the pandemic,
COVID-19, audio hearings were scheduled after giving prior notice to both the
parties.



The Appellant did not participate in the hearing since he refused to take the call
despite a couple of attempts.



Respondent is represented by Shri S.Roy Biswas, PIO/Dy. Secy-Inter Cell
through audio conference. He submitted that the instant RTI Application was
filed by the Appellant for seeking marks of his wife Smt. Pooja Devi. He further
submitted that the exam for post code 15/17 for the post of Special Educator
does not have Part A and Part B and the said exam had the criteria to pass the
exam separately after which the marks are generated separately to ascertain
whether the candidate has qualified individually in both the exams or not. In
addition, he also submitted that the list of the successful candidates has been
displayed on their website roll-number wise but the marks can be accessed only
by the respective candidates by logging into the respective portal.



He further added that the instant RTI Application was received upon transfer on
09.04.2019 and the Appellant was informed vide letter dated 09.04.2019 that
the information sought pertains to third party hence, the same is exempt from
disclosure as per the provisions of the 
RTI Act
. He added that the decision of the
PIO was also upheld by the First Appellate Authority.



## Decision:


Upon perusal of the facts on records as well as on the basis of the submissions
of the Respondent public authority, Commission observes that the information
sought in the instant RTI Application pertains to third party and the same is
exempt from disclosure under 
Section 8(1)(j)
 of the RTI Act. Since the PIO has
already informed the Appellant vide reply dated 09.04.2019 and the Appellant
has not appeared to buttress his case/demonstrate the cause of dissatisfaction
with the information provided by the Respondent public authority, no further
adjudication is warranted in the instant matter.

cannot be supplied being third party information.



Dissatisfied with the reply received from the PIO, the Appellant filed a First
## Appeal dated 12.04.2019, which was decided by the FAA vide order dated

with the instant Second Appeal.
