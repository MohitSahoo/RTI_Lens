## ORDER


                                    (03.06.2020)


1.        The issues under consideration arising out of the second appeal dated
21.02.2018 include non-receipt of the following information raised by the appellant
through his RTI application dated 07.12.2017 and first appeal dated 25.01.2018:-

     i.   Please provide the date wise and amount wise information of Diem Allowance
          paid to on monthly basis by Bank of Baroda, on account of deputation from
          Hardoi branch to Regional Office, Lucknow, during the period from

                                                                              Page 1 of 6

       18/06/2010       to     31/03/2011,     along     with      the     copy       of
      circular/settlement/Regulation, showing the relevant clause of my above
      entitlement, under which, above Diem Allowance was paid to me and also the
      name of the authorities who have verified/recommended/paid the same to me.



2.    Succinctly facts of the case are that the appellant filed an application dated
07.12.2017 under the 
Right to Information Act, 2005
 
(RTI Act
) before the Central
Public Information Officer (CPIO),Bank of Baroda, Regional Office, Gomtinagar,
Lucknow, seeking aforesaid information. The CPIO replied on 10.01.2018.
Dissatisfied with the response of the CPIO, the appellant filed first appeal dated
18.01.2018. The First Appellate Authority disposed of the first appeal vide order
dated 25.01.2018. Aggrieved by this, the appellant has filed a second appeal dated
21.02.2018 before this Commission which is under consideration.



3.    The appellant appellant filed the instant appeal dated 21.02.2018 inter alia on
the grounds that the CPIO refused to provide full, correct and true information to the
RTI application. Moreover, the First Appellate authority did not provide the appellant
with an opportunity for personal hearing despite specific request made by the
appellant. The appellant requests the Commission to direct the CPIO to provide
complete, correct and true information and also invoke 
Section 20(2)
 of the RTI Act
against the First Appellate Authority.



4.    The CPIO vide letter dated 10.01.2018 refused to provide information to the
appellant citing that the information sought for by the appellant was not within the
jurisdiction of their branch. The FAA vide his order dated 25.01.2018 agreed with the
views taken by the CPIO.

## Hearing on 04.12.2019:


4.1.   The appellant and on behalf of the respondent Shri Somesh Nag Bhushan,
Senior Manager, Bank of Baroda, Lucknow, attended the hearing through video
conference.

4.2.   The Commission passed the following directions on 12.12.2019:

       "6. The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
       hearing both the parties and perusal of records, feels that the reply given by the
       respondent is perfunctory and the appellant having sought information relating to
       his own allowances is entitled to receive complete information. The reply given is
       evasive and incomplete. A period of more than two years has elapsed and respondent
       have not provided the information sought and rules under which they acted upon. In
       view of this, the registry of this bench is directed to issue show cause notice to the
       present CPIO Shri A.K. Singh as well as to the then CPIO Shri P.K. Sachdeva to
       show cause as to why a maximum penalty of Rs.25,000/- may not be imposed upon
       each of them for obstruction of information. All written submissions must reach this
       Commission within 21 days."

Hearing on 21.04.2020


5.     The appellant and on behalf of the respondent, Shri A.K. Singh, CPIO and Shri
Somesh Nagbhushan, Sr.Manager (Legal), Bank of Baroda, Lucknow attended the
hearing through teleconference.

5.1.   The appellant inter alia submitted that the diem allowance amount was
recovered from his bank account with remarks reflecting that the same as overdraft.
The appellant had sought information with respect to his diem allowance, however,
the respondent did not provide the requisite information. The appellant insisted that




                                                                                   Page 3 of 6

 maximum penalty be imposed upon the concerned CPIOs for the unreasonable delay
caused in the matter.

5.2.   The respondent submitted that detailed written explanation in response to the
show cause notice dated 12.12.2019 was submitted to the Commission vide their
letter dated 30.12.2019. The respondent stated the appellant during the pendency of
second Appeal, Appellant Shri Praveen Bhatnagar also filed a writ petition before
Hon'ble High Court, Lucknow. The decision in the said writ petition was passed on
30.07.2018 and as per the direction of the Hon'ble High Court, the amount was paid
to Shri. Praveen Bhatnagar setting the controversy to an end. The respondent further
submitted that as regard to the name of authorities who verified and recommended
for payment of Diem to Shri Praveen Bhatnagar has already been mentioned in para 8
of the judgment of Hon'ble High Court at page no. The bank's infact did not hide the
information and the information was available to the Appellant as mention in the
## order. It was also appraised that the Appellant who was bank own employee and

bank accommodated him on his request also dragged bank into litigation despite
good gesture and consideration given to the employee. The matter was sub judice and
payment has been released to the Appellant in terms of order of Hon'ble High Court
setting the controversy to an end. The respondent Shri P.K. Sachdeva, the then CPIO,
did not submit any written explanation in his defense.

6.     The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case,
hearing both parties and perusal of records, feels that the respondent even in response
to the show cause notice have not provided the information sought by the appellant
through their reply dated 30.12.2019. The CPIO has consistently referred to different
correspondences and documents vide which they have claimed to have provided the
information earlier, however, they have not produced a detailed point-wise
information to the points raised in the RTI application. This not only establishes non-
compliance of the Commission's order dated 12.12.2019 but also makes it clear that
the respondent have violated the provisions of the 
RTI Act
 in terms of unreasonable

                                                                             Page 4 of 6

 delay caused in the matter. Shri A.K. Singh have not cited a single reason explaining
the delay of around two and a half years. Shri P.K. Sachdeva who was the erstwhile
CPIO had the primary responsibility to respond within 30 days of receipt of the RTI
application. The then CPIO had given an evasive reply that the application was not
concerning their branch instead of transferring the RTI application to such
department/branch. The information sought by the appellant was all along in their
custody, however, the same was not disclosed for unknown reasons till a period of
two and a half years from date of RTI application was elapsed. The delay caused in
this matter reflects the callous attitude and ignorance of the then CPIO towards RTI
applicants as well as customers and that had the matter been dealt with properly the
same might have not escalated upto this level. They have further adopted delaying
tactics to avoid disclosure of the information requested, relating to the appellant's
own account. The explanations submitted by the CPIO Shri A.K. Singh, who
delegated a subordinate officer to represent him during the penalty proceedings, are
unsatisfactory and unreasonable to the extent that not a single reason for the delay
caused was brought out in their written as well as oral submissions. It is also noted
that no explanation has been furnished on behalf of the then CPIO.
6.1.   The Commission notes that the negligence of duty as designated CPIOs
appears to be deliberate and mala fide is established on part of both Shri A.K. Singh
and Shri P.K. Sachdeva, hence, both are found liable as per 
section 20 (1)
 of RTI
Act. In view of this, a penalty of Rs. 15,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand only) may be
imposed on Shri P.K. Sachdeva, the then CPIO and penalty of Rs. 10,000/- (Rupees
Ten Thousand) Rs. 10,000/- may be imposed on Shri A.K. Singh, present CPIO. Rs.
15,000/- shall be deducted from the salary of Shri P.K. Sachdeva, the then CPIO, (in
three equal instalments) and Rs. 10,000/- shall be deucted from salary of Shri A.K.
Singh, present CPIO (in two instalments), by the Public Authority and paid by way of
demand draft drawn in favour of "PAO, CAT", New Delhi, forward the demand
drafts addressed to the Deputy Registrar (CR-II), email: 
[email protected]
