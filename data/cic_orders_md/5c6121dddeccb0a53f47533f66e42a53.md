                        CIC/NDMCH/A/2018/135853

The Appellant filed RTI application dated 27.03.2018seeking information
## through 11 points:


   1. Status of his complaint dated 27.02.2018 addressed to the Commissioner,
      North DMC, regarding the unauthorized construction as well as action taken
      report on the same.
   2. Whether property has been booked?
   3. If the property has been booked, the notings of the booking on the file as
      well as the action taken after booking under the 
DMC Act
.
   4. Whether NOC from the concerned Municipal Corporation has been taken for
      the construction of the said building.
   5. Whether building has been constructed after fulfilling the Delhi bye-laws.
   6. Action taken on the concerned Junior Engineer, whose name has been
      mentioned in the complaint for carrying out the alleged unauthorized
      construction etc.

Having not received any reply from the PIO, Appellant filed First Appeal dated
27.04.2018. PIO/EE/Vig./NDMC, vide letter dated 18.05.2018 informed the
Appellant that his Complaint dated 27.02.2018 was received in Vigilance
Department on06.03.2018 vide Central Dy. No. 2177. As per the order of Higher
Authority, the same was forwarded to Dy. Commissioner KBZ on 09.03.2018 for
taking appropriate action as deem fit at their end.

Having not received any response from the FAA, Appellant approached the
Commission in the instant Second Appeal
## Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:



Respondent alone is present for the hearing while the Appellant has not
attended hearing despite service of hearing notice in advance. Respondent
submitted that as per directions of FAA, the Department vide letter dated
28.06.2018 requested the Deputy Commissioner/Karol Bagh zone to provide
requisite information/action taken report on the averred complaint, directly to
the Complainant/Appellant. He also placed a copy of written submission from
PIO/Vig.SDMC dated 02.03.2020, before the Commission for the perusal of the
Commission. The Appellant is not present to buttress his case.



## Decision:


Considering the facts that emerge from the records, it is noted that FAA had
passed an order dated 22.06.2018 and directed the then PIO/Vigilance to advise
Zonal Officials concerned to provide information against the complaint filed by
the Appellant and obtain factual report from Dy. Commissioner/Karol Bagh
Zone, which remained non-complied till date.



In view of the above, the Commission hereby directs PIO/Karol Bagh Zone to
comply with the directions of First Appellate Authority and furnish reply to the
Appellant within 4 weeks from the date of receipt of this order under intimation
to the Commission. It is made clear that non-compliance of the directions will
attract penal provisions.



The appeal is thus disposed of with the above directions.

## Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:


Respondent alone is present for hearing while the Appellant has not attended
hearing despite service of hearing notice in advance. Respondent has submitted
that information as sought by the Appellant in both the cases has been duly
furnished and that the First Appellate Authority had also upheld the same.



## Decision:


Perusal of records of the case reveals that complete information has already
been provided to the Appellant. Since information as sought has been furnished
and the Appellant has not appeared to buttress the case, it is not possible to
ascertain the cause of dissatisfaction with the information provided. No further
cause of action remains to be adjudicated in these cases.



The appeals are thus disposed of with no further direction.

## Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:


 Respondent alone is present for hearing while the Appellant has not attended
 hearing despite service of hearing notice in advance. Respondent has submitted
 that a complaint was received on 25.05.2018 and the same was forwarded to
 the Deputy Commissioner, North MCD, Karol Bagh as the complaint falls within
 the jurisdiction of North MCD. The Appellant was also informed that the matter
 has been forwarded to North MCD for further necessary action. The Appellant is
 not present to buttress his case.



## Decision:


 Since the matter has already been transferred by the PIO/SDM(PN) to Deputy
 Commissioner, North MCD/Karol Bagh, the Commission directs the Deputy
 Commissioner, North MCD/Karol Bagh Zone to provide an action
 taken/compliance report in this regard before the Commission by 10.04.2018,
 failing which appropriate legal action shall be initiated against the erring official
 found causing obstruction in the dissemination of information.



 The appeal is disposed off with the above directions.
