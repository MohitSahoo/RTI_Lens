## DECISION:




 Keeping in view the facts of the case and the submissions made by both the parties and in the light
 of the decisions cited / observations made in the preceding paragraphs, as also taking into
 consideration the ease and convenience of the stakeholders at large, the Commission directs the Pr.
 Commissioner cum Secretary, DDA to prepare a centralized database of the contractors who have
 been awarded contracts by all divisions / departments and suomoto disclose the information on
 their website in accordance with the provisions of 
Section 4
 of the RTI Act, 2005 within a period of
 30 days from the date of receipt of this order in the larger publicinterest.



 The Commission further observed that the centralized process of receiving the RTI applications and
 transferring the same to the concerned department was not streamlined and was far from satisfactory
 in DDA. Therefore, taking strong exception to the flagrant violation of the provisions of the 
RTI
 Act
 and its spirit as also the instructions issued by the DoP&T from time to time, the Commission
 observes that there is urgent need to evolve a robust and effective RTI mechanism to ensure that
 correct and timely information is provided to the information seekers. The action taken report to
 ensure compliance with the aforementioned observation of the Commission should be sent by the
 Pr. Commissioner and Secretary, DDA to the Commission under intimation to the Appellant within
 a period of 30 days from the date of receipt of this order failing which proceedings
 u/s 20 (1)
 of the
 Act could beinitiated.



 The Commission also instructs the Respondent Public Authority to convene periodic
 conferences/seminars to sensitize, familiarize and educate the concerned officials about the relevant
 provisions of the 
RTI Act, 2005
 for effective discharge of its duties and responsibilities.



 The Appeal/ Complaint stands disposed accordingly.
