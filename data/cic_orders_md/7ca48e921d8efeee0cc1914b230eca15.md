 Having not received any reply from the PIO, Appellant filed First Appeal dated

## Facts emerging in Course of Hearing:



The Appellant participated in the hearing on being contacted on her telephone.
She stated that the information sought in both these matters is identical and
that no satisfactory response was provided by the Respondent. She further
stated that despite visiting the office of the Public Authority no information was
disclosed by the Respondent. It appears that duplicate second appeals have
been filed in this matter.



The Respondent is represented by Shri Gurucharan Singh, DCA- Misusethrough
audio conference. He stated that the information held and available with them
was provided to the Appellant and that an opportunity of hearing was also
granted to the Appellant at the First Appeal stage which was not availed by her.
The Respondent however agreed to furnish a detailed written submission
explaining the factual position in the matter to the Commission.



Subsequent to the hearing the Commission is in receipt of a written submission
from the PIO/ DCA, D/o Architecture dated 14.09.2020 wherein it was inter alia
stated that an RTI No 577 dated 08.08.2018 was sent to the concerned officials
of the NDMC i.e., the PIO (EBR), PIO (Estate-II), DCA (Design) and EE (BP) for
their reply/ information as per their records. After receiving the information/
replies from the DCA (Design) and EE (BP), the same was conveyed to the
applicant vide letter dated 30.08.2018. The PIO/ Dy Dir (Estate-II) and PIO
(EBR) had directly replied to the Appellant vide letter dated 21.08.2018 and
10.09.2018 respectively. Thereafter the Appellant filed a First Appeal on
11.10.2018 and the FAA had called the Appellant for hearing on 30.11.2018 at
12:00 PM. The Appellant did not reach the office of the FAA at the scheduled
time hence on the request of the Appellant the hearing was rescheduled on
06.12.2018 at 11:30 AM which was also informed to the Appellant. The
Appellant preferred not to attend the hearing on 06.12.2018 and consequently
filed a Second Appeal before the Commission. With regard to the RTI application
no 647 dated 29.08.2018, the Respondent stated that the said application was
received in the Centralised RTI Cell NDMC and the same was sent to different
departments of NDMC but inadverantly the same might have been clubbed with
other files/ PUCs and therefore was nottraceable. After receiving the appeal
dated 11.10.2018, it was observed that the information asked in this RTI
application was the same as the RTI application no 577 dated
08.08.2018therefore the FAA had called the Appellant for hearing on
30.11.2018 at 12:00 PM. While re-iterating the aforementioned action taken
subsequent to the fixing the hearing of the first appeal, the Respondent
requested that the aforementioned RTI application may be remanded back to
Shri Rajeev Sood, FAA/ Chief Architect, NDMC for giving a hearing to the
Appellant with the directions to the PIO (EBR), PIO (Estate-II) to attend the
hearing for issuing detailed necessary directions to all the concerned officials to
provide the requisite information as per their records in a time bound manner.



                                                                          Page 2 of 5


 On perusal of the available records, the Commission is of the view that no
substantive point wise reply has been provided to the Appellant, till date. The
only document enclosed by the Respondent which contains a substantive
response is a reply of the PIO/ Dy Director (Estate-II) dated 21.08.2018 replied
against the RTI application dated 08.08.2018 wherein the information sought is
denied u/s 11 (1) being third party information. However, the said reply pertains
to an RTI application dated 08.08.2018 which is not the subject matter of the
instant second appeal. As regards the instant matter, the Respondent has
stated that the RTI application was received in the centralised RTI Cell NDMC
and sent to different department of NDMC but inadverantly the same might
have been clubbed with other files/ PUCs and therefore was not traceable. The
Respondent has also averred that the opportunity of hearing provided by the
FAA is not availed by the Appellant.



## Decision:


Keeping in view the facts of the case and the submissions made by both the
parties and in the light of the fact that the First Appeal filed by the Appellant
has not been decided, till date, the Commission deems it fit to remand the
instant case to the FAA and Chief Architect, NDMC, Architecture Department, to
provide a fair hearing to both the parties and pass a reasoned, speaking order,
copies of which should be sent to the Appellant and the Commission by
13.10.2020.



With the above directions, the instant Second Appeal stands disposed off.
