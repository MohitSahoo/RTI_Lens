## FACTS:


The Appellant/ Complainant vide his RTI application sought information on 14 points regarding
the civil works (through work service order) done or completed in April, 2016 till 25th July, 2017
and were also paid during the aforementioned period, civil works done and completed during the
aforementioned period but not paid and issues related thereto.



The CPIO and Ex Engineer North Division No 5, DDA vide its letter dated 06.11.2017 provided
a point wise response to the Appellant/ Complainant. Dissatisfied by the response, the Appellant/
Complainant approached the FAA. The FAA, vide its order dated 17.11.2017 while stating that a

                                                                                       Page 1 of 9

 separate warning memo had been issued to the PIO and Ex Engineer, Civil ND-5 directed the
CPIO to fix a site visit with the Appellant/ Complainant in order to redress his grievance. In
compliance with the order of the FAA, the CPIO vide letter dated 25.11.2017 provided a
response to the Appellant/ Complainant subsequent to the inspection of records availed by him.



## HEARING:


## Facts emerging during the hearing:


## The following were present:


Appellant/ Complainant: Mr. Akshay Kumar Malhotra;

Respondent: Mr. Shiv Kumar Garg, EE / ND-5 and Mr. B. B. Gulati, AE (Consultant);



The Appellant/Complainant reiterated the contents of the RTI application and stated that at the
outset he would like to inform the Commission that the previous decisions of the Commission on
public interest matters like this had not been complied with. He also submitted that the nature of
the information sought was such that it should have been suo moto disclosed on the website of
the Public Authority instead of providing the same while replying to an RTI application and that
a reply in itself did not constitute information under the 
RTI Act, 2005
. In the instant matter, he
essentially desired the information regarding the contractor to whom payment was made / not
made for the work performed by them in the period mentioned in the RTI application. While
going through point-wise replies furnished by the CPIO he expressed his dissatisfaction on
points No. 01, 02 and 07. In its reply, the Respondent reiterated the submissions of the CPIO /
FAA and stated that point-wise information was provided by the CPIO / FAA informing him
about the total payment made for the work performed during the period mentioned in the RTI
application as also the details of contracts where payments were not made. However, the
Appellant remained dissatisfied as the scope of work had not been clearly specified.



The Commission was in receipt of a written submission from the Appellant/ Complainant dated
19.12.2019 wherein it was inter alia stated that the RTI application was in the larger public
interest and should have been disseminated in public domain by the public authority at their own.

While alleging that the CPIO and FAA don't disseminate the public information at their own,
provide information under the RTI applications and First Appeals, the Appellant/ Complainant
stated that the compliance of the orders of the Commission was merely reduced to submission of
a reply. The Appellant also submitted that the CPIO/ FAA were chronic defaulters of the
provisions of the 
RTI Act, 2005
 and directions of the Commission in recent past which he listed
in his reply. He also stated that the prayer of the Commission was the same as the respective
## Complaints and Second Appeals.

With regard to the imposition of penalty on the CPIO/PIO under Section 20 of the RTI Act,
2005, the Commission took note of the ruling of Hon'ble Delhi High Court in W.P.(C)
11271/2009 Registrar of Companies & Ors v. Dharmendra Kumar Garg & Anr. (delivered on:

## 01.06.2012) wherein it was held:




         " 61. Even if it were to be assumed for the sake of argument, that the view taken by the
# learned Central Information Commissioner in the impugned order was correct, and

        that the PIOs were obliged to provide the information, which was otherwise retrievable
        by the querist by resort to 
Section 610
 of the Companies Act, it could not be said that
        the information had been withheld malafide or deliberately without any reasonable
        cause. It can happen that the PIO may genuinely and bonafidely entertain the belief
        and hold the view that the information sought by the querist cannot be provided for one
        or the other reasons. Merely because the CIC eventually finds that the view taken by
        the PIO was not correct, it cannot automatically lead to issuance of a showcause notice
        under 
Section 20
 of the RTI Act and the imposition of penalty. The legislature has
        cautiously provided that only in cases of malafides or unreasonable conduct, i.e., where
        the PIO, without reasonable cause refuses to receive the application, or provide the
        information, or knowingly gives incorrect, incomplete or misleading information or
        destroys the information, that the personal penalty on the PIO can be imposed. This
        was certainly not one such case. If the CIC starts imposing penalty on the PIOs in every
        other case, without any justification, it would instill a sense of constant apprehension in
        those functioning as PIOs in the public authorities, and would put undue pressure on
        them. They would not be able to ful fill their statutory duties under the 
RTI Act
 with an
        independent mind and with objectivity. Such consequences would not auger well for the
        future development and growth of the regime that the 
RTI Act
 seeks to bring in, and
        may lead to skewed and imbalanced decisions by the PIOs Appellate Authorities and
        the CIC. It may even lead to unreasonable and absurd orders and bring the institutions
        created by the 
RTI Act
 in disrepute."

## DECISION:




Keeping in view the facts of the case and the submissions made by both the parties, the
Commission directs the CPIO to re-examine the RTI application and furnish further details on
points 01, 02 and 07 of the RTI application to the Appellant/ Complainant with enclosures within
a period of 15 days from the date of receipt of this order.



The Commission also instructs the Respondent Public Authority to convene periodic
conferences/seminars to sensitize, familiarize and educate the concerned officials about the
relevant provisions of the 
RTI Act, 2005
 for effective discharge of its duties and responsibilities.



The Appeal/Complaint stand disposed accordingly.
