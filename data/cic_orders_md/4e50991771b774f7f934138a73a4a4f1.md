          "
Section 8(1)(e)

Section 8(1)(1)
 of the Right to Information Act, 2005 exempts the disclosure
of personal information that is not in the public interest or that would invade
an individual's privacy."

The information sought by the appellant cannot be provided to him as this
## information sought by him is protected under Section (8) (1) (e) of the Act and

no public activity or interest involved in this information and this information
as sought by the appellant in his application at Sr. No.1 and 2 relates to the
personal interest and no public element involved in it and as such same cannot
be provided to him under the ibid Act.

The Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in the case titled as 
Canara Bank Vs.
G.S. Shyam
 bearing Civil Appeal No. CA-22 of 2009, decided on 31.8.2007
has held that information where no public element is involved cannot be
provided under the 
RTI Act
. So, as per this judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme
Court of India and an per the provisions of 
RTI Act
, information sought by the
appellant in his application at Sr. No.1 and 2 cannot be provided to the
appellant as it does not involve any public interest and this information also
protected under fiduciary relationship and same cannot be provided U/s 8 (1)


(e) of the Act.

4. That the information sought by the appellant in its application dated
25.11.2024 at Sr. No. 3. 5 and 6 rightly declined by the CPIO vide its reply
dated 9.12.2024. The information cannot be provided to the appellant, as
authority cannot be compelled to produce the document, which does not
pertain to him. This document sought by the appellant at Sr. No. 3. 5. 6 does
not pertain to the concerned office of the CPIO and same pertains to the SAI
Head Office, New Delhi and as such the CPIO cannot be directed to produce
the said document. This view finds support from the judgment of the Hon'ble
Supreme Court in the case of 
CBSE vs Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors
, Civil
## Appeal No.6454 of 2011, whereby their Lordship has held as below:



"At this juncture, it is necessary to clear some misconceptions about the 
RTI
Act
. 
The RTI Act
 provides access to all information that is available and
existing.



                                                                              Page 4 of 9


        This is clear from a combined reading of 
section 3
 and the definitions of
       'information' and 'right to information" under clauses and of 
section 2
 of the
       Act. If a public authority has any information in the form of data or analyzed
       data, or abstracts, or statistics, an applicant may access such information,
       subject to the exemptions in 
section 8
 of the Act. But where the information
       sought is not a part of the record of a public authority, and where such
       information is not required to be maintained under any law or the rules or
       regulations of the public authority, the Act does not cast an obligation upon
       the public authority, to collect or collate such non-available information and
       then furnis it to an applicant....."

       In view of this judgment of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India the information
       sought by the appellant at Sr. No. 3. 5. 6 cannot be provided to the appellant.

       5.That now regarding the information sought by appellant at Sr. No. 7 to 9

       6. Now, regarding the other information as alleged by the appellant in the

## Decision:




8.     The Commission after adverting to the facts and circumstances of the case, hearing
both parties and perusal of records, observes that the main premise of instant Second
## Appeal/Complaint is non-furnishing of information by the Respondent.




9.     Commission notes that the Appellant/Complainant was working as a junior
consultant in Netaji Subash National Institute of Sports, Patiala and was terminated from
service w. e. f 14.11.2024. It is observed that the Appellant/Complainant has sought
information related to his termination and matters related thereof. It is observed that at
points No. 1 and 2 the Appellant/Complainant has sought copies of file notings and

                                                                                    Page 6 of 9

 approvals of the competent authority pertaining to the letters issued to him prior to his
termination from service. Considering the fact that the information sought directly relates
to the termination of the Appellant/Complainant and has a direct bearing on his legal rights,
the Commission is of the considered view that disclosure of such information is in the
interest of justice. Therefore, copies of the approvals granted by the appropriate/competent
authority for issuance of the said letters ought to be furnished to the
Appellant/Complainant. With regard to the file notings, it is observed that the remarks,
opinions, and recommendations recorded by officers/officials on the relevant files can be
disclosed after duly severing or redacting the names, or any other particulars which may
reveal the identity of the concerned officers/officials or any other third-party. Such
severance shall be carried out strictly in accordance with 
Section 10
 of the Right to
Information Act, 2005.



10.   Accordingly, the PIO is directed to furnish a revised reply in respect of Points No.
1 and 2, i.e. specifically provide copies of the approvals of the competent authority
obtained prior to issuance of the letters in question and copies of relevant file notings after
redacting third party information or any other information which is exempted under

Section 8
 and 
9
 of the RTI Act 2005, in compliance with 
Section 10
 thereof, within four
weeks from the date of receipt of this order. A compliance report in this regard shall be
filed a week thereafter.



11.   As regards points No. 3, 5 and 6 the PIO, Netaji Subhash National Institute of Sports,
Patiala has stated that the information sought pertains to their Head Office Delhi. It is
pertinent to mention that the Respondent has neither transferred the said point of the RTI
Application under 
Section 6 (3)
 of the RTI Act nor obtained the relevant information
under 
Section 5 (4)
 of the RTI Act from the concerned custodian of the records i.e. their
Head Office, Delhi. At this belated stage the Commission is not willing to entertain any
request which would further delay the disclosure of the information.



12.    Now the stage of transferring the RTI Application has passed and hence, PIO shall
procure a reply with respect to points No. 3, 5 and 6 from the custodian of information and
provide it to the Appellant. If the Respondent requires assistance from any other

                                                                                     Page 7 of 9

 office/officer for compliance with the above directions, the same shall be sought by
invoking 
Section 5(4)
 of RTI Act. Accordingly, Commission deems it fit to direct Dr.
Rajbir Singh, Deputy Director, Netaji Subhash National Institute of Sports, Patiala to
invoke the provisions of 
Section 5 (4)
 of the RTI Act and furnish a revised reply with
respect to points No. 3, 5 and 6 of the RTI Application within four weeks from the date of
receipt of this order. A compliance report in this regard shall be filed a week thereafter.

The Second Appeal No. CIC/SAOIN/A/2025/608536 is disposed of accordingly.



13.   Regarding the Complaint No. CIC/SAOIN/C/2024/655850, it is observed that the
records of the case do not indicate any such deliberate denial or concealment of
information on the part of the CPIO, hence no action is warranted under 
section 18
 and

20
 of the RTI Act. Therefore, the aforementioned Complaint is disposed of, accordingly.
