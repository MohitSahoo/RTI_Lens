## Query Assistant / Optimizer Tests

1. **Vague Query**: 
   "I want information about railways."

2. **Specific but poorly phrased**: 
   "Why did the Ministry of Finance reject my application about GST refunds in 2023? I think they are being unfair."

3. **Complex Legal Query**: 
   "Records concerning the internal protocols for Section 8(1)(a) exemptions in the Ministry of Defence during the fiscal year 2024-25."

4. **Document Request**: 
   "Copies of all correspondence between MEA and the Passport Office regarding the delay in passport issuance for the month of March 2024."

## Q&A / RAG Tests

1. **Exemption Inquiry**: 
   "What are the common grounds for denial under Section 8(1)(j) for personal information?"

2. **Precedent Search**: 
   "Find CIC orders where the Ministry of Railways was directed to disclose recruitment details."

3. **Process Question**: 
   "How can I file a second appeal to the CIC if my first appeal was rejected by the FAA?"

4. **Section 8(1)(h) Inquiry (Investigation/Prosecution)**:
   "Under what circumstances can a department deny information under Section 8(1)(h) regarding pending investigations?"

5. **Fiduciary Exemption Search (Section 8(1)(e))**:
   "Can a bank refuse to share transaction details of a borrower under the fiduciary relationship exemption of Section 8(1)(e)?"

6. **Third Party Information (Section 11)**:
   "What is the procedure followed by the CPIO when disclosure of third-party information under Section 11 is requested?"

7. **Public Activity vs. Personal Privacy**:
   "In what scenarios does the public interest override the Section 8(1)(j) exemption for public officials' assets and assets disclosure?"

8. **Section 24 Exclusion (Security/Intelligence)**:
   "Are organizations listed under the Second Schedule (Section 24) completely exempt from RTI, or are there exceptions for corruption and human rights violations?"
